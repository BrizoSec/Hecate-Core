## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are exploiting a malicious Twitch browser extension that has exposed OAuth tokens from approximately 30,000 users. The compromised tokens are being transmitted to a bot service located in Russia, enabling unauthorized access to user accounts.

### Key Findings
- Malicious browser extensions represent a significant threat vector for stealing credentials and OAuth tokens.
- The stolen OAuth tokens allow adversaries to gain unauthorized access to Twitch user accounts without further interaction from the victim.
- Adversaries are directing compromised data to bot services hosted in Russia, suggesting possible state-sponsored or coordinated cybercrime activities.
- [UNCERTAIN] The exposure of 30,000 users' OAuth tokens implies a large-scale compromise and potentially broad access to user accounts.
