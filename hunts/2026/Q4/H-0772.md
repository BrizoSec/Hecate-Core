### Justification

The PoeLLM campaign specifically targets exposed AI services (LiteLLM, Ollama) and routers to deploy XMRig/Iron miners. The unique tradecraft involves using filenames like 'libgcrypt' and 'python3' for malicious ELF binaries to evade signature detection, and deriving C2 IPs from GitHub poems to bypass static IP blocklists. Hunting for these specific file drops and outbound connections to known mining infrastructure (Kryptex) is critical because standard network monitoring fails to detect the dynamic C2 resolution, and endpoint detection is hindered by the legitimate-looking filenames.
