This indicates the adversary is using Windows Management Instrumentation (WMI) to query remote systems for running processes, likely to identify targets for lateral movement or to verify the presence of specific security tools. Hunting for this specific WMI usage pattern is critical as it represents active reconnaissance and potential lateral movement in an environment where Cobalt Strike was previously observed.
