### Justification

This hunt addresses a critical post-exploitation capability where root access on a K8s node allows identity spoofing within the service mesh. By manipulating cgroup metadata, an attacker can trick the SPIRE agent into issuing valid JWTs for other services, effectively bypassing mTLS authentication and potentially enabling lateral movement (capability; not observed in the source) or privilege escalation within the cluster. This is high-risk because it undermines the core trust model of SPIFFE/SPIRE deployments without leaving traditional network-based traces.
