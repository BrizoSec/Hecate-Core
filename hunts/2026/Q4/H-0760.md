### Justification

The Lunex campaign specifically exploits CVE-2023-20598 in the AMD PDFWKRNL.sys driver to manipulate kernel callbacks, effectively blinding endpoint security products without stopping them. This allows the subsequent deployment of LunexStealer to harvest credentials from browsers and crypto wallets. Hunting for this specific BYOVD technique is critical because standard EDR alerts may be suppressed or missed if the driver appears legitimate, and the persistence mechanisms (scheduled tasks) provide a reliable detection vector even if the initial exploit is stealthy.
