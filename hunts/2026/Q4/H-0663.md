### Justification

The threat intel explicitly describes a 'ClickFix' tactic where spear-phishing emails and compromised wiki pages redirect victims to fake CAPTCHA screens. These screens coerce users into copying and pasting PowerShell commands that download and execute the LegionLoader backdoor. This is a high-risk initial access vector targeting Windows workstations, leading directly to credential theft (Chrome profiles) and further payload delivery. Hunting for this specific user-initiated PowerShell execution pattern is critical because it bypasses traditional email filtering by relying on social engineering and legitimate system binaries.
