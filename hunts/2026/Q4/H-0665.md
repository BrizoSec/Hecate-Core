### Justification

Star Blizzard (SEABORGIUM) has evolved its RedFlick technique to streamline malware delivery via single-click LNK files that leverage legitimate tools (conhost.exe, cmd.exe, ssh.exe) for evasion. This hunt targets the specific abuse of SSH's PermitLocalCommand feature and the creation of scheduled tasks with benign names ('Internet Quality Test Connection', 'Network Configuration Manager') to persist and execute the CosmicPulse downloader, which is critical given the actor's focus on credential theft and cyberespionage against high-value targets.
