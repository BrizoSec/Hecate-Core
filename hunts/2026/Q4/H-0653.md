### Threat Context

Source CTI:

> Title: SocGholish Campaign Targeting Washington State Election Infrastructure
Source: Threat Intelligence Report

Description:
A targeted phishing campaign has been observed directing malware at election officials and political organizations in Washington state ahead of the November elections. The campaign utilizes electorally themed phishing emails containing malicious zip attachments or links to look-alike domains impersonating the Washington Secretary of State's office.

Upon execution, the payload delivers the SocGholish (FakeUpdates) framework. The malware uses JavaScript embedded in compromised websites or delivered via HTML smuggling to profile the victim's system. It executes obfuscated WScript commands to download a secondary payload. The primary goal appears to be establishing initial access into networks that house voter registration databases and electronic pollbook management systems. 

Indicators suggest the threat actors are also conducting reconnaissance for potential DDoS attacks against election websites, looking to overwhelm them on election day to erode public confidence. The malware achieves persistence by creating scheduled tasks named "UpdateCheck" and modifies registry run keys.

Key Indicators:
- Suspicious domains: wa-elections-support.com, secure-voter-wa.org
- WScript executing obfuscated JavaScript from %APPDATA%\Local\Temp
- Scheduled task creation: schtasks /create /sc minute /mo 15 /tn "UpdateCheck"
- Malicious IPs observed in C2 traffic: 198.51.100.45, 203.0.113.88
