### Justification

The threat intelligence explicitly describes a 'ClickFix' campaign targeting Ukrainian and international victims. The attack relies on social engineering (fake CAPTCHA) to trick users into copying a malicious command to their clipboard and pasting it into the Windows Run dialog. This behavior results in the execution of `msiexec` to download and install the 'Psychedelic' infostealer, which targets cryptocurrency wallets and browser credentials. Hunting for this specific sequence is critical because it bypasses traditional email-based phishing filters and leverages user interaction to execute code, directly impacting confidentiality and integrity of financial and credential data.
