### Justification

This behavior directly enables the actor's goal of maintaining stealthy, long-term access across enterprise environments by leveraging commodity infrastructure (e.g., YouTube lures, SEO poisoning) and evasion techniques such as domain rotation, steganography, and browser hijacking. The use of legitimate-looking domains (e.g., blogspot burners, typosquats like crowdstri.com) and obfuscated C2 communication (e.g., Base64 + XOR decoding) reduces detection likelihood. The campaign’s multi-payload delivery via OfferLoader and subsequent deployment of Insomnia RAT, ARKTunnel, and Docro Hijacker indicates a focus on persistence, command-and-control, and data monetization (via search hijacking), all of which pose significant risk to enterprise confidentiality and integrity.
