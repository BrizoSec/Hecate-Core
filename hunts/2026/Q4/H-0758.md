### Justification

The Cling botnet specifically targets embedded Linux/IoT devices (routers, DVRs) by exploiting command injection vulnerabilities (e.g., CVE-2021-35394). It could establish persistence (capability; not observed in the source) via hidden files and binary replacement (wget hijacking) and uses STUN protocol traffic to Google's public infrastructure to evade detection. Hunting for this behavior is critical because standard network monitoring often fails to distinguish malicious STUN payloads from legitimate NAT-traversal, allowing persistent C2 channels to remain active undetected on vulnerable edge devices.
