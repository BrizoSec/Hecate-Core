### Justification

The threat intelligence explicitly details the TraderTraitor group's campaign involving a trojanized Terraform provider that downloads loaders from lookalike domains (e.g., hashicorp-terraform.io). This is not generic malware but a targeted supply-chain attack against infrastructure-as-code developers. Hunting for this specific behavior is critical because standard endpoint protection may miss the initial download if it blends with legitimate developer tooling traffic, and the subsequent persistence mechanisms (services like 'snap-imagent' or 'powershell-config-service') are designed to survive reboots and evade casual inspection. The use of encrypted executables in fake font files further complicates detection, requiring deep file inspection and behavioral monitoring rather than just static hash matching.
