### Justification

The BengalSEO operation leverages SEO poisoning to rank malicious lure pages at the top of search results, particularly on Bing. These pages redirect users through a rotating TDS using domains like ts.remdos.com and to.ghredir.com [the source does not tie it to this intrusion (unknown)], which are hosted on infrastructure shared with Matomo analytics at stats.us3.org. This system enables precise targeting and fingerprinting of victims, allowing the attackers to deliver MayaBot malware or funnel users into tech support scams. Detecting this behavior is critical because it represents a sophisticated, persistent campaign that exploits legitimate-looking web infrastructure and search engine manipulation to compromise users at scale, directly aligning with the organization’s risk exposure to web-based social engineering and malware delivery vectors.
