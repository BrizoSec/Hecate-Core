### Justification

This hunt targets a sophisticated credential theft campaign where attackers bypass traditional malware detection by serving a genuine, digitally signed WatchGuard installer from an official CDN. The primary risk is not the installation of the software itself, but the exfiltration of VPN credentials via browser pop-ups to a C2 domain (watchguard-ssl.online). Detecting this requires correlating network connections to the phishing infrastructure with subsequent legitimate downloads from cdn.watchguard.com, as standard endpoint detection may miss the threat due to the valid signature of the final payload.
