### Justification

The 'Once in a BlueMoon' campaign (APT31/TA412) utilizes a novel exploit chain targeting Chrome V8 and Windows kernel vulnerabilities to deliver the GemStone browser extension. This extension could perform surveillance and credential theft (capability; not observed in the source), often followed by ShadowPad deployment. Hunting for this specific behavior is critical because standard endpoint detection may miss the initial browser-based persistence (GemStone) until C2 activity occurs, and the use of legitimate-looking update mechanisms (ChromeUpdate.exe) can evade basic file integrity checks.
