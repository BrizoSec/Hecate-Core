### Justification

The NeedyMantis framework, attributed to the Storm-3069 threat actor, specifically employs modular loaders and encrypted archives to maintain post-compromise access. By dropping malicious DLLs (e.g., WinSparkle.dll, libcurl.dll) into non-standard directories like %ProgramData% subfolders, the malware evades standard detection while leveraging legitimate-looking file names. Hunting for these specific artifacts and their associated C2 communications is critical because this TTP indicates an active, persistent intrusion (target sector unconfirmed by the source), rather than opportunistic malware.
