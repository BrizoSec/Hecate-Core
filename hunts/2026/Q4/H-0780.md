### Justification

The .conf26 incident demonstrates that attackers leverage trusted CDN nodes (Amazon CloudFront) to host malicious domains, making simple IP blocking ineffective due to collateral damage on legitimate services like Samsung Health. Hunting for this behavior is critical because standard IP reputation checks will flag the entire CDN node as malicious or benign depending on the threshold, leading to either service disruption or missed detections. By focusing on specific domain names resolved from that IP, defenders can isolate the compromised host without affecting the broader user base.
