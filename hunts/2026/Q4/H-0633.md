### Justification

The source intelligence details a specific intrusion campaign involving BumbleBee and Akira ransomware where the threat actor employed sophisticated evasion techniques. Specifically, they used 'AdgNsy.exe', a renamed instance of the legitimate Windows Address Book utility, injected with shellcode, and utilized DLL sideloading via 'icardagt.exe' dropping 'version.dll'. Hunting for these specific process names and file artifacts is critical because standard EDR rules may miss them if they are not explicitly flagged as suspicious due to their legitimate parent processes or names. The presence of these artifacts indicates active C2 communication and preparation for ransomware deployment.
