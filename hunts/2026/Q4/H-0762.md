### Justification

The threat intel explicitly describes a known adversary tradecraft (KMON_NOC platform) that automates the validation of stolen AWS keys by calling STS GetCallerIdentity followed by Amazon Bedrock API calls (ListFoundationModels, Converse/InvokeModel). This behavior is distinct from generic credential stuffing because it specifically probes for LLM access and promotional credits to determine resale value. Hunting for this specific sequence could allow detection of credential harvesting (capability; not observed in the source) operations that are actively monetizing stolen infrastructure before deeper exploitation occurs.
