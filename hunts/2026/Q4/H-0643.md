### Justification

The Atomic macOS (AMOS) Stealer campaign specifically targets macOS users by masquerading as legitimate installation instructions. The threat intel confirms a multi-stage delivery mechanism involving the download of Zsh scripts from staging domains (getmacouscloud.com, ferncore13.com) and the execution of Mach-O binaries in /tmp and /Library/Application Support. Hunting for these specific artifacts is critical because standard endpoint monitoring often misses hidden files (like .service) in Application Support directories, and generic network logs may not distinguish stealer beaconing from legitimate API metrics calls to domains like grove-89.com.
