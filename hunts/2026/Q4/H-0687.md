### Justification

The NightEagle APT group specifically targets Russian enterprises by compromising valid VPN credentials (often via Cloudflare WARP or European infrastructure) to bypass perimeter defenses. Once inside, they deploy the GhostContainer backdoor on Microsoft Exchange servers, exploiting CVE-2020-0688 to inject malicious payloads into ASP.NET VIEWSTATE parameters. This could allow them to maintain persistence and tunnel lateral movement (capability; not observed in the source) traffic (using rdp2tcp and Microsoft Dev Tunnels) through standard web ports, evading traditional network monitoring. Hunting for these specific artifacts is critical because the group focuses on deep infrastructure dominance, including DCSync attacks against Active Directory.
