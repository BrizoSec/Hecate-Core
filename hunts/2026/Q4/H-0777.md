### Justification

The MATCHBOIL malware family, attributed to UAC-0099, specifically evolves its persistence mechanisms from registry keys to scheduled tasks (e.g., Updates\CheckTask, UpdateCheckers\DailyPlanner) and drops payloads in user-specific directories like %LOCALAPPDATA% and Pictures. Hunting for these specific artifacts is critical because they represent the active installation phase of the malware, allowing defenders to detect the presence of the MATCHWOK backdoor before lateral movement or data exfiltration occurs.
