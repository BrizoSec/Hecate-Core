### Justification

The 'Psychedelic Stealer' campaign specifically exploits the 'ClickFix' social engineering technique, where victims are tricked into manually executing `msiexec.exe` commands. This hunt is critical because the adversary deliberately uses signed Microsoft binaries to bypass standard detection heuristics that typically flag encoded PowerShell or unsigned scripts. By focusing on process execution logs for `msiexec.exe` with suspicious command lines and subsequent file creation of `psychedeliclove.exe`, we can detect this specific delivery mechanism before credential theft occurs.
