### Justification

The ClingSTUN campaign specifically targets internet-facing Linux/IoT devices, exploiting unpatched vulnerabilities for initial access. The malware's distinct behavior involves abusing public STUN servers for NAT traversal to establish reliable Command and Control (C2) channels and disguising its process name as 'init' to evade detection. Hunting for these specific indicators is critical because standard network monitoring often ignores UDP 3478 traffic, and endpoint visibility frequently misses PID 1 child processes unless explicitly audited. This hunt addresses the gap in detecting this specific persistence and C2 mechanism used by this known threat actor.
