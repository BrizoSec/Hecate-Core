## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
Passkeys are a form of strong authentication mechanism that uses public-key cryptography to provide users with a secure, passwordless login experience. Passkeys typically rely on biometric or device-specific factors for user verification and are designed to enhance security by eliminating the need for traditional passwords.

### Key Findings
- Passkeys utilize public-key cryptography to ensure secure authentication without requiring users to remember complex passwords.
- Biometrics and device-specific factors play a crucial role in verifying the identity of the user during passkey authentication processes.
- The primary goal of passkeys is to enhance security by reducing reliance on traditional password mechanisms, which are often vulnerable to various types of attacks.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are leveraging passkey-themed social engineering tactics to trick individuals into revealing their identity and cloud credentials, exploiting the growing reliance on passkeys for authentication. By posing as trusted entities or using phishing techniques, attackers can gain unauthorized access to user accounts and potentially compromise entire cloud environments.

### Key Findings
- Passkey-themed social engineering often involves phishing emails or web pages designed to mimic legitimate services that support passkeys.
- [UNCERTAIN] Attackers may use pretexting calls or SMS-based attacks to gather initial information before launching targeted spear-phishing campaigns.
- Once an individual falls for the deception, adversaries can exploit stolen credentials to gain unauthorized access to cloud accounts and resources.
- Compromised cloud environments allow attackers to perform further exfiltration of sensitive data, lateral movement within networks, or establish persistent footholds.
- [UNCERTAIN] Social engineering tactics may evolve alongside advancements in passkey technology to maintain their effectiveness against increasingly savvy users.
