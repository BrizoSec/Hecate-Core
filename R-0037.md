## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
AI platforms are designed to process large volumes of data and generate insights or predictions that can be used for various applications such as natural language processing (NLP), computer vision, and predictive analytics. These systems often rely on machine learning models trained with extensive datasets to improve accuracy and efficiency.

### Key Findings
- Threat actors may exploit vulnerabilities in AI platforms' data inputs, leading to biased or incorrect outputs that can be used for malicious purposes.
- [UNCERTAIN] Malicious actors could manipulate training data or use adversarial attacks to degrade model performance, potentially undermining the integrity of decisions made by these systems.
- AI platforms typically lack robust mechanisms to detect and mitigate sophisticated attacks, making them susceptible to exploitation through various vectors such as input injection or model theft.
