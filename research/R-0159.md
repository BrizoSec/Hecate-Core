## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are leveraging various Denial of Service (DoS) techniques to disrupt services, including amplification attacks and reflected spoofed attacks that exploit network protocols to generate large volumes of traffic. These tactics overwhelm the target's infrastructure and can result in service unavailability.

### Key Findings
- Amplification-attack techniques are being used to exponentially increase the volume of traffic directed at a victim through vulnerable servers.
- Reflected-spoofed-attacks involve sending large amounts of traffic from spoofed IP addresses, making it difficult for defenders to trace and mitigate attacks.
- Slow-read-attack is employed to degrade web service performance by initiating many connections but reading data very slowly, exhausting server resources over time.
- Flooding-attack methods are being utilized to overwhelm network bandwidth or server capacity with a high volume of traffic, causing denial of services for legitimate users.
