## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are increasingly leveraging prompt injection techniques to manipulate AI-based systems like CrowdStrike's Falcon AIDR, enabling them to evade detection and execute malicious activities undetected. By injecting carefully crafted prompts, attackers can alter the behavior of AI models used in cybersecurity solutions, undermining their effectiveness.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries are using prompt injection techniques to manipulate AI-driven security tools such as CrowdStrike's Falcon AIDR.
- Attackers craft specific input patterns (prompts) that trigger unintended behaviors or vulnerabilities within the AI model.
- These attacks often aim to bypass detection mechanisms by altering the context in which the AI operates, making it harder for traditional signatures and heuristics to identify malicious activities.
- CrowdStrike is expanding its services to include proactive measures against such AI-based threats, indicating a growing awareness of this method among threat actors.
