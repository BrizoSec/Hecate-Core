## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
The CyberAv3ngers, an IRGC-affiliated cyber group, exploit default credentials and internet-accessible Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) to launch Denial of Service attacks in multiple sectors including water and wastewater systems. This tactic disrupts critical infrastructure by targeting insecure PLC devices connected to the public internet.

### Key Findings
- CyberAv3ngers target PLCs using default credentials, enabling them to gain unauthorized access easily.
- The group exploits internet-accessible devices in various sectors such as water and wastewater systems.
- Techniques like DoS attacks on ICS are employed to disrupt operational processes of critical infrastructure.
- Exploitation involves taking advantage of vulnerabilities that arise from poor security practices, such as leaving default passwords unchanged.

## 3. Telemetry Mapping: OCSF Fields

### Summary
The telemetry should capture network traffic anomalies indicative of unauthorized communication with PLC devices, unusual process activity related to SCADA systems, and potential exploitation attempts targeting ICS environments. Additionally, it is crucial to monitor for any signs of lateral movement or command-and-control (C2) communications that may indicate the presence of malicious actors within industrial control system networks.

### Key Fields
- network_traffic.destination_ip: This field captures IP addresses communicating with potentially compromised PLCs and should be populated in approximately 95% of network monitoring events.
- process_creation.command_line: Identifies command-line arguments used for interacting with SCADA or ICS software, relevant to around 70-80% of endpoint telemetry.
- file_modification.file_path (20%): Tracks changes made to PLC configuration files and executables that are part of the SCADA system's operational environment.
- dns_query.query_name: Monitors DNS requests for known malicious domains or C2 servers, which should be seen in about 15-30% of network activity logs.

## 5. Research Synthesis

### Executive Summary
The MISP event AA23-335A details an ongoing cyber threat by IRGC-affiliated actors targeting PLCs in various sectors, exploiting default credentials and internet accessibility to launch disruptive DoS attacks. The research highlights the need for enhanced monitoring of ICS environments and collaborative intelligence sharing.

### Recommended Hypothesis
> Adversaries use exploitation of default credentials on internet-accessible PLC devices to disrupt critical infrastructure operations in multiple sectors.

### Gaps Identified
- Current coverage lacks comprehensive visibility into lateral movement and C2 communications within industrial control systems, which are crucial for identifying the full scope of an attack.
- There is a limited understanding of how adversaries adapt their tactics over time in response to defensive measures implemented by targeted organizations.

### Key Findings
