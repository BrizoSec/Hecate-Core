## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries often leverage compromised systems to establish persistence, move laterally within networks, exfiltrate data, and cover their tracks. IOCs for 2026-08-02 would highlight specific malware signatures, IP addresses associated with command-and-control servers, or unusual network traffic patterns indicative of ongoing attacks.

### Key Findings
- Advanced persistent threats (APTs) are increasingly using artificial intelligence to automate and personalize their attack vectors.
- Malware distribution through supply chain attacks is on the rise, targeting software updates and patches to deliver malicious payloads.
- Lateral movement techniques involving living-off-the-land binaries (LOLBins) continue to be prevalent, leveraging legitimate system tools for nefarious purposes without downloading suspicious files.
- Data exfiltration methods are becoming more sophisticated with adversaries using encryption and steganography to evade detection while transferring stolen data out of compromised networks.
