## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
Zimbra mail servers are targeted by threat actors exploiting XSS vulnerabilities to execute JavaScript without user interaction, leading to credential harvesting and data theft. The exploitation primarily involves manipulating email content to trigger these vulnerabilities.

### Key Findings
- CVE-2024-45519 is a critical remote command execution vulnerability in Zimbra that has been exploited by threat actors targeting government, defense, transportation, and financial organizations across NATO member states and Ukraine.
- The `msg_receiver` function within the Zimbra mail server's codebase processes SMTP commands like `RCPT TO:<...>` to store recipient addresses, which can be manipulated to exploit XSS vulnerabilities.
- Russian and Belarussian threat actors, notably TA488 ('Laundry Bear'), have shifted their tactics from exploiting Zimbra to targeting Outlook Web Access (OWA) in recent attacks.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries target Zimbra mail servers using half-click exploits to automate the exfiltration of sensitive data, leveraging phishing and malicious attachments to gain initial access. Once compromised, they encode and transmit stolen data through various channels like DNS queries and HTTPS.

### Key Findings
- Phishing campaigns are used to trick users into clicking on malicious links or opening attachments that compromise Zimbra accounts.
- Encoded JavaScript is employed to facilitate the exfiltration of sensitive information from compromised servers.
- Data exfiltration occurs via multiple channels, including DNS A-record queries and HTTPS uploads for larger payloads.
- The threat actors use traffic duplication as a sub-technique under T1020 to mimic legitimate network activity and evade detection.
- Compromised email accounts are repurposed by attackers to spread the malicious campaign further within networks.
- Adversaries adapt their tactics, moving from targeting Zimbra servers to exploiting similar vulnerabilities in other mail systems like Outlook.
