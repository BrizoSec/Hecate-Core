## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries exploit AI systems through prompt injection techniques, manipulating input prompts to induce unintended behavior from AI models. CrowdStrike's involvement in the Open Secure AI Alliance aims to mitigate these risks by advancing industry standards and collaboration on AI safety.

### Key Findings
- Prompt injection attacks are becoming more sophisticated, allowing attackers to manipulate AI responses to disclose sensitive information or perform unauthorized actions.
- Adversaries use social engineering tactics alongside technical exploits to deceive users into submitting malicious prompts that can trigger specific vulnerabilities in AI systems.
- New prompt injection taxonomy from CrowdStrike provides a comprehensive framework for understanding and mitigating these threats, aiding security teams in developing defensive strategies.
- AI models are increasingly integrated with cloud environments, expanding the attack surface and necessitating robust third-party exposure management solutions like Falcon Exposure Management.
- CrowdStrike's AIDR tool helps organizations secure their AI attack surfaces by detecting and responding to potential threats more effectively.
- Industry collaboration through initiatives such as the Open Secure AI Alliance is critical for advancing the safety and security of AI systems against emerging threats.
