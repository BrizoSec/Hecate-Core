## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
SANDWORM_MODE is a sophisticated supply chain attack that targets AI toolchains by injecting malicious code into critical components like MCP servers and leveraging zero-width Unicode poisoning to compromise systems. This campaign, active since February 2026, uses coordinated multi-vector attacks and exploits the unique vulnerabilities of AI frameworks without relying on traditional CVEs.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries inject malicious code into Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers to gain control over AI toolchains and steal credentials.
- Supply chain attacks are conducted through popular package managers like npm, PyPI, and development environments such as VS Code.
- Zero-width Unicode poisoning is used to compromise files like .cursorrules and CLAUDE.md, leading to unauthorized access and further exploitation.
- Coordinated multi-vector campaigns leverage AI orchestration and autonomous agents for broader attack coverage and deeper system infiltration.
