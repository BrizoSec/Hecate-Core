## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
Shopify's Shop app uses a notification system to alert users of order and invoice-related updates. Scammers are exploiting this system by sending fake refund alerts that appear legitimate, tricking users into providing remote access which they then use to steal money.

### Key Findings
- Scammers exploit Shopify's notification pipeline to send fake refund notifications within the app.
- Victims receive notifications about refunds for returned items or canceled services and are prompted to contact a number for assistance.
- Remote access software like AnyDesk is used by scammers once they gain control of the victim’s device.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Scammers are exploiting Shopify's notification system within its Shop app to send fake refund alerts and invoices, tricking users into calling fraudulent numbers or sharing personal information. This technique leverages the legitimacy of Shopify’s notifications to bypass user skepticism typically associated with phishing attempts.

### Key Findings
- Scammers register their own stores or take over legitimate ones to create fake orders for targets.
- The scam involves sending out fake refund alerts through the official Shop app notification system, which appears authentic and trustworthy to users.
- Victims are pressured into calling phone numbers provided in these notifications, leading them into further phishing attempts where personal information is solicited or funds requested.
- This method abuses Shopify's own infrastructure, making it difficult for users to distinguish between genuine alerts and fraudulent ones.

## 5. Research Synthesis

Scammers are exploiting Shopify's notification system within its Shop app to send fake refund alerts, tricking users into providing remote access and personal information. This sophisticated phishing technique leverages the legitimacy of Shopify’s notifications to deceive victims.

### Recommended Hypothesis
