## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
Hackers-for-hire groups typically operate by identifying vulnerabilities in systems, conducting reconnaissance to understand target networks, and deploying various tactics such as phishing emails, malware distribution, and exploiting software weaknesses to gain unauthorized access for espionage or financial gain. In the case of cryptocurrency fraud, attackers may use techniques like creating fake wallets or exchanges to steal user funds.

### Key Findings
- Espionage attacks usually involve detailed reconnaissance followed by targeted exploitation of system vulnerabilities.
- Cryptocurrency fraud often leverages social engineering tactics and exploits trust in legitimate systems to deceive victims into revealing their private keys or accessing compromised platforms.
- Hackers-for-hire groups are known to offer a range of services, including but not limited to data theft, surveillance, and disruption of operations for financial compensation.
