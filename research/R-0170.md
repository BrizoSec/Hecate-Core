## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries frequently exploit phishing techniques to obtain credentials from victims by leveraging localized, managed campaigns. Once initial access is gained through these methods, further credential theft can be facilitated via brute force or credential dumping.

### Key Findings
- Phishing remains a primary method for adversaries to acquire user credentials and gain unauthorized access to systems.
- Localized and managed phishing campaigns are increasingly effective as they tailor attacks to specific regions or organizations.
- Successful phishing often leads to further credential theft through brute force or credential dumping techniques.
- SMS-based phishing (smishing) is used to trick victims into installing malware or navigating to malicious websites.
- Multi-factor authentication codes sent via SMS can be intercepted and abused by adversaries, highlighting the importance of additional security measures.
- Implementing multi-factor authentication significantly reduces the risk associated with stolen credentials.
