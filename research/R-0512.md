## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
Cl0p ransomware operates by infiltrating systems, typically through phishing emails or exploiting vulnerabilities, then encrypting files while also potentially stealing sensitive information to leverage against the victim for ransom demands.

### Key Findings
- Cl0p often uses initial access brokers to gain entry into networks before deploying its malware.
- [UNCERTAIN] Cl0p frequently targets large organizations with substantial resources and is known to leak stolen data on the dark web if a ransom isn't paid.
- The group behind Cl0p has been observed using various tactics, such as lateral movement within compromised networks, to maximize their impact.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Cl0p ransomware actors typically gain initial access through phishing or exploiting vulnerabilities in remote desktop services. Once inside, they use lateral movement to identify and encrypt valuable data, often exfiltrating data before initiating encryption to apply additional pressure on victims via data leaks.

### Key Findings
- Initial Access: Cl0p operators commonly exploit weak RDP configurations or spear-phishing campaigns to gain initial foothold in the network.
- Lateral Movement: Once inside, adversaries use tools like PsExec and WMI for lateral movement across the network to identify critical systems with valuable data.
- Data Exfiltration: Before initiating encryption, Cl0p actors typically exfiltrate a copy of the targeted data as an additional coercive tactic if ransom demands are not met.
- Encryption: The primary payload is the Cl0p ransomware which encrypts files across multiple machines using strong encryption algorithms to make recovery without decryption impossible.
- Data Leak Threats: After launching attacks, Cl0p often threatens to leak stolen data unless ransoms are paid, increasing pressure on victims to comply.
- [UNCERTAIN] Use of Living-off-the-Land Binaries: While not specific to Cl0p, there is a pattern of using built-in system tools for stealthy operations which may be employed by this group.

## 5. Research Synthesis

### Executive Summary
Cl0p ransomware actors typically infiltrate networks through phishing or exploiting weak RDP configurations, moving laterally within the network to encrypt valuable data and exfiltrate sensitive information. They leverage initial access brokers and use tools like PsExec and WMI for lateral movement before deploying their ransomware payload.

### Recommended Hypothesis
> Adversaries use lateral movement and data exfiltration techniques to deploy Cl0p ransomware on large organizations with substantial resources, aiming to maximize the impact of their attack through encryption and subsequent data leaks.
