## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
BengalSEO is a black hat SEO operation that employs various techniques to poison search results and redirect victims to scam pages or malware-infested sites. It uses a Traffic Distribution System (TDS) for filtering traffic and delivering targeted payloads, often bypassing security measures like CAPTCHA through sophisticated fingerprinting methods.

### Key Findings
- BengalSEO utilizes black hat SEO techniques to manipulate search engine results and direct victims to scam pages or malware delivery sites.
- The Traffic Distribution System (TDS) is employed as a gating mechanism to filter traffic and deliver specific payloads based on the victim's characteristics.
- The operation incorporates sophisticated fingerprinting and CAPTCHA bypass methods to identify and redirect targeted users.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
BengalSEO leverages Black Hat SEO techniques to manipulate Bing search results, directing victims to phishing pages that deliver malware such as MayaBot. The campaign uses tactics like backlinks and keyword stuffing to poison search outcomes.

### Key Findings
- BengalSEO exploits search engine optimization (SEO) poisoning to redirect users to malicious content.
- The operation employs techniques including backlink generation and keyword stuffing to manipulate Bing's search algorithms.
- Phishing pages hosted on legitimate-looking domains like readthedocs.io are used to distribute malware.
- A Linux toolkit is deployed for remote control and data theft purposes.
