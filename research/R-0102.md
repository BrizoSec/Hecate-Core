## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are increasingly exploiting NTLM (NT LAN Manager) vulnerabilities in Active Directory environments to perform credential theft and lateral movement within networks. This exploitation often involves sophisticated techniques such as the use of adversary-in-the-middle tactics on mobile devices, where malicious applications can intercept traffic by posing as legitimate network clients.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries are leveraging NTLM weaknesses to carry out pass-the-hash (PtH) attacks and lateral movement within compromised Active Directory environments.
- Credential theft remains a favored tactic among adversaries, often achieved through the exploitation of NTLM relay attacks or by using malware that intercepts network traffic for credential harvesting.
- Mobile devices are targeted via adversary-in-the-middle techniques where malicious apps register as trusted VPN clients to redirect traffic and exfiltrate data or inject payloads into network communications.
- The use of AI-driven security platforms like CrowdStrike’s Falcon Platform is critical in detecting and mitigating such advanced threats that employ complex attack vectors.
