## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
Illicit online casino domains serve as a multifaceted threat vector, simultaneously facilitating illegal gambling, transnational money laundering via 'scambling' withdrawal fraud, and hosting command-and-control infrastructure for espionage-linked malware frameworks like PeckBirdy. These malicious operations exploit the visual similarity between legal and illegal gambling sites to evade detection while leveraging bulletproof hosting in Asia and infrastructure laundering through U.S./European providers.

### Key Findings
- Over 1.7 million Chinese-language casino domains are identified as facilitating illegal gambling and transnational money laundering, primarily operated by triad-aligned syndicates utilizing deposit bonuses to lure victims into rigged games.
- China-aligned APT groups deploy the PeckBirdy malware framework within fake casino and adult websites as command-and-control infrastructure, achieving zero detection rates on VirusTotal and targeting education, government, finance, and technology sectors across Asia.
- Adversaries employ 'scambling' techniques where thousands of websites target global audiences with rigged games and withdrawal fraud, while simultaneously laundering infrastructure through U.S. and European hosting providers to maintain bulletproof Asian hosting.

The PeckBirdy malware framework and associated illicit gambling infrastructure exploit the visual uniformity of illegal casino domains to mask command-and-control traffic, leveraging zero-detection evasion techniques on VirusTotal while utilizing infrastructure laundering across US/EU hosting providers. This campaign effectively blends transnational money laundering operations with state-aligned espionage campaigns, targeting high-value sectors like finance and government through rigged 'scambling' platforms that serve as initial access vectors.

### Recommended Hypothesis
> Adversaries use legitimate-looking Chinese-language casino domains hosted on laundered US/EU infrastructure to deploy the PeckBirdy malware framework for espionage and money laundering, achieving zero detection rates on standard threat intelligence feeds.
