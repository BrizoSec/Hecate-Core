This collaboration aims to foster a community dedicated to defending against evolving cybersecurity challenges posed by advanced AI technologies.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries may exploit vulnerabilities in AI systems to launch sophisticated attacks, such as prompt injection techniques, which manipulate user input to deceive or control the system's output.
