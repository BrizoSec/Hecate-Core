## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries with privileged network access employ adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) attacks to intercept and modify network traffic in real time, enabling them to block, log, alter, or inject data. This technique is commonly executed through ARP poisoning or deploying proxy servers.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries often utilize ARP poisoning as a method to launch AiTM attacks by creating false entries in the target's ARP cache.
- Proxy servers are another tool employed to intercept network traffic, allowing adversaries to manipulate data before it reaches its destination.
- Indicators of compromise (IoCs) for AiTM attacks can include unusual network traffic patterns or unexpected changes in routing information.
- Detection mechanisms should focus on monitoring for unauthorized man-in-the-middle (MITM) activities and ensuring secure communication channels are used.
