## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries may abuse AI infrastructure by deploying sophisticated tactics such as exploiting vulnerabilities in the training data, model architecture, or deployment environment. They can also use deception techniques like adversarial examples to degrade performance and gain unauthorized insights into AI operations.

### Key Findings
- Attackers frequently use social engineering and phishing emails to gain initial access to an organization's network where AI systems are deployed.
- [UNCERTAIN] Adversaries may leverage deepfake technology or similar techniques to create synthetic data that can confuse AI models during the training phase, leading to biased outcomes.
- Commonly observed tools include malware like keyloggers and backdoors which allow attackers persistent access to the network where AI infrastructure resides.
- Adversaries often employ distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks on cloud-based AI services to disrupt operations temporarily or permanently.
- Indicators of compromise might include unexpected spikes in resource consumption, unusual data transfers, or unauthorized API calls from unfamiliar IP addresses.
- Attackers frequently target misconfigurations and weak credentials as initial entry points into environments hosting sensitive AI assets.
