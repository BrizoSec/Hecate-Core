Operation RoundPress involves the exploitation of zero-day vulnerabilities in webmail systems, specifically those that can be triggered with minimal user interaction (half-click).

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Operation RoundPress leverages zero-day vulnerabilities in webmail applications to exploit public-facing systems, allowing adversaries to gain unauthorized access and potentially intercept multi-factor authentication mechanisms. This enables them to maintain persistence within the network and escalate privileges.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries are using sophisticated techniques to identify and exploit software weaknesses in publicly accessible webmail services.
- The interception of multi-factor authentication (MFA) provides attackers with a method to bypass traditional security controls, facilitating unauthorized access.
- Web shells may be deployed on compromised servers as part of the attack chain to maintain persistent access and control over affected systems.
- Vulnerability scanning and active reconnaissance are employed by adversaries to discover exploitable weaknesses in Internet-facing applications.
