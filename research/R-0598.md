## 3. Telemetry Mapping: OCSF Fields

### Summary
To detect advanced threats leveraging AI and automation, the telemetry should capture indicators of process injection, lateral movement, credential theft, and suspicious network communications. This includes monitoring for unusual process invocation patterns, unexpected service creation/deletion, and irregular DNS requests.

### Key Fields
- process_creation: Indicates when a new process is started on the endpoint (70% populated): Monitors the execution of programs or scripts that could be part of an attack chain such as PowerShell or cmd.exe.
- file Modification: Reflects changes in file attributes, permissions, or content (50% populated): Helps detect unauthorized modifications to critical system files or executables.
- network_connection: Tracks outbound and inbound network connections made by processes (60% populated): Detects unusual traffic patterns that may indicate data exfiltration or command-and-control communication.
- user_account_activity: Logs actions taken by users, including login attempts and privilege changes (45% populated): Useful for identifying unauthorized access or credential misuse.
