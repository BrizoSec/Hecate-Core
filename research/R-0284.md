## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are utilizing advanced AI technologies to rapidly identify vulnerabilities and exploit them before traditional defenses can react. This necessitates that defenders adopt more sophisticated approaches such as those offered by frontier AI to stay ahead in the cybersecurity arms race.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries are using AI to automate and accelerate attack strategies, collapsing the window of opportunity for defensive measures.
- CrowdStrike is collaborating with OpenAI to develop advanced threat detection methodologies that can counteract AI-driven attacks.
- The integration of frontier AI in cybersecurity enhances predictive analytics, enabling proactive defense rather than just reactive response.
- DNS poisoning remains a critical attack vector, and AI is being used both offensively by adversaries and defensively by security teams to mitigate such risks.
