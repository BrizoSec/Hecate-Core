### Summary
Adversaries exploit AI systems through techniques like prompt injection to manipulate or bypass security measures, leading to unauthorized access and data breaches. CrowdStrike has identified over 180 different methods of prompt injection, emphasizing the evolving nature of these threats.

### Key Findings

- CrowdStrike tracks more than 180 distinct prompt injection methods, indicating the breadth and diversity of such attacks.
- The development of a new Prompt Injection Taxonomy by CrowdStrike provides security teams with detailed insights into how these attacks operate and can be detected.
