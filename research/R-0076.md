## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
JadeProx is a cyber operation linked to China that was exposed due to an operational security (OPSEC) lapse, specifically the use of compromised infrastructure with identifiable characteristics. This incident highlights how adversaries can inadvertently reveal their activities through such mistakes.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries may use similar infrastructure or IP addresses for multiple operations, leading to cross-referencing and potential exposure if one operation is discovered.
- Operational security lapses such as using identifiable command-and-control (C2) servers can lead to the detection of larger cyber espionage campaigns.
- The JadeProx incident demonstrates how threat intelligence platforms like OTX are crucial in sharing indicators of compromise (IOCs) that help track and mitigate threats across organizations.
- Tools used by adversaries, such as proxies or compromised hosts for C2 communications, can be traced back to the original source if there is a breach in OPSEC.
