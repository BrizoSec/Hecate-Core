## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are leveraging AI to accelerate their operations and evade detection more effectively, leading to a significant increase in the pace of cyber attacks. This arms race compels security teams to continuously innovate and respond faster than attackers to defend enterprise systems.

### Key Findings
- AI is being used by adversaries to compress attack timelines and obfuscate malicious activities, making it harder for defenders to detect and mitigate threats.
- There has been an 89% annual increase in AI-enabled adversary operations, indicating a rapid escalation in the sophistication of cyber attacks.
- Adversaries are employing new techniques such as Adversary-in-the-Middle (AiTM) attacks to intercept and manipulate network traffic in real time.
- Security teams must focus on enhancing their ability to detect obfuscation tactics employed by AI-powered adversaries, which can obscure malicious intent.
- Enterprise systems that rely heavily on AI may become prime targets for sophisticated cyber threats due to the increased complexity and speed of modern attacks.
