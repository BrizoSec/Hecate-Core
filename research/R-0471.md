## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries leverage the Adversary-in-the-Middle (AiTM) technique, T0830 from MITRE ATT&CK framework, to intercept and manipulate network traffic in real-time by exploiting privileged network access. This approach enables attackers to alter communication between parties without being detected.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries use AiTM attacks to modify data in transit, allowing them to inject malicious content or block legitimate communications.
- Attackers often gain initial access through phishing campaigns that trick users into executing malware, thereby establishing a foothold for further network exploitation.
- Monitoring for IoAs such as unusual traffic patterns and anomalies in SSL/TLS certificates can help detect AiTM activities before IoCs become evident.
- Tools like Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) proxies or ARP spoofing software are commonly employed to facilitate the interception of network communications.
