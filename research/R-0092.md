This includes monitoring for unusual outbound SMTP traffic, unexpected inbound IMAP or POP3 connections, and anomalous logins or changes in mailbox permissions.

### Key Fields
- email_server_events (90% populated): Logs of all incoming and outgoing emails, including headers, attachments, and metadata. This helps in identifying malicious payloads and suspicious email patterns.

- network_traffic_email_protocol (70% populated): Logs capturing SMTP, IMAP, and POP3 traffic to monitor for unusual activities such as bulk email sending or data exfiltration attempts.
- authentication_events (85% populated): Tracks login attempts and failures across all systems that may be targeted via phishing emails, aiding in identifying credential compromise events.
