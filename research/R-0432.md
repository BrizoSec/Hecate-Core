### Key Findings
- Adversaries are likely to target open-source AI models with newly discovered vulnerabilities, potentially compromising security initiatives.
- Shared tools and frameworks for enhancing cybersecurity may be reverse-engineered by adversaries to understand defensive strategies better and develop countermeasures.
- Real-world threat intelligence can also become a double-edged sword if it falls into the wrong hands; adversaries might use this data to refine their attack vectors against AI systems or entities involved in the alliance.
- Collaborative efforts aimed at improving AI safety could attract disinformation campaigns from state-sponsored actors seeking to disrupt collective progress.
- The integration of third-party environments through Falcon Exposure Management can introduce new entry points for sophisticated adversaries aiming to breach networks.
