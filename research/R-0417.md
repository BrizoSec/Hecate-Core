### Summary
Adversaries may abuse Falcon Onum's advanced detection capabilities by employing sophisticated techniques that can evade regex-based CQL patterns and AI-driven behavioral analysis, such as using polymorphic malware or obfuscating shell commands to avoid signature-based detections.

### Key Findings
- Sophisticated adversaries use polymorphic malware to evade static signatures like those detected by Falcon Onum's regex-capable correlation engine.
- Adversaries increasingly employ command-line obfuscation techniques on systems like VMware ESX to bypass detection mechanisms, including those in Falcon Onum.
- Behavioral analysis within Falcon Onum can be challenged when attackers mimic legitimate user behavior patterns, making it difficult to distinguish between benign and malicious activities without additional context.
- Third-party EDR tools integrated with Falcon Next-Gen SIEM may introduce inconsistencies or gaps that adversaries could exploit to hide their activity from comprehensive threat hunting efforts.
