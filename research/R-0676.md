## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
Executive Threat Detection (ETD) is a proactive, intelligence-led service provided by Cisco Talos Incident Response designed to protect high-value IT assets associated with specific executives. It operates through a dedicated team of experts conducting monthly custom threat hunts and generating reports on the corporate systems of up to 10 principals.

### Key Findings
- ETD provides protection for a maximum of 10 principals, focusing specifically on their high-value IT assets and corporate systems.
- The service relies on two distinct hunting methodologies: Baseline Threat Hunting, which involves deep-dive reviews of events and telemetry to identify anomalies, and Emerging Threat Hunting, which applies atomic and pattern-based indicators derived from monthly OSINT reviews.
- The primary operational goal is the proactive detection of compromises involving executive-associated assets before they can be leveraged for broader organizational breaches.
- The service is delivered as part of a retainer offering, utilizing human-led threat hunting and intelligence analysis to address gaps in standard protection mechanisms.
