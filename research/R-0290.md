## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are increasingly adopting AI to enhance their capabilities, enabling them to automate and scale attacks at unprecedented speeds. This shift necessitates advanced cyber defense strategies that can detect and respond to AI-driven threats in real-time.

### Key Findings
- A significant year-over-year increase of 89% in attacks by AI-enabled adversaries highlights the growing threat landscape.
- CrowdStrike's Falcon platform uses real-time indicators of attack, enriched threat intelligence, and evolving adversary tradecraft to counteract these advanced threats.
- Continuous identity verification for both human operators and AI agents is critical as attackers leverage AI to impersonate legitimate users or entities more convincingly.
- Security teams need to be aware of new AI-driven tools like OpenClaw, which can act as super-agents in orchestrating complex attacks across multiple fronts.
- The widening governance gap around Shadow AI presents both opportunities and risks for defenders and attackers alike.
