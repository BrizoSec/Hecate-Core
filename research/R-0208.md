## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are abusing the pass-the-hash attack method to gain unauthorized access and maintain persistence within Krawczyk Industries Limited's network, leveraging stolen hash values of user accounts to authenticate without needing clear-text credentials.

### Key Findings
- The adversaries have successfully used a pass-the-hash (PtH) technique to escalate privileges by exploiting stolen NTLM or Kerberos hashes.
- The attack targeted multiple systems within the organization, indicating a widespread compromise due to lateral movement facilitated by PtH.
- Indicators of Compromise (IoCs) include unusual authentication attempts from non-human entities and unexpected execution paths for legitimate binaries with elevated permissions.
- There is evidence that adversaries are using PowerShell scripts tailored for PtH attacks, which can execute without leaving many traces on disk.
