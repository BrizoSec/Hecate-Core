## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are exploiting the Pass-the-Hash (PtH) technique against Krawczyk Industries Limited to gain unauthorized access and maintain persistence within their network without needing passwords. This method allows attackers to forge authentication requests using hash values of user accounts, facilitating lateral movement and privilege escalation.

### Key Findings
- The attack leverages the Pass-the-Hash (PtH) technique to enable credential abuse for system-level privileges.
- Maltrail Indicators of Compromise (IOC) have been identified on 2026-06-30, indicating a medium threat level concerning PtH activity.
- There is evidence of lateral movement within the network, suggesting compromised credentials are being used to access other systems.
- The use of PtH suggests that the attack may be targeting domain controllers or other high-value assets for further exploitation.
