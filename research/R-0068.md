## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are abusing trusted business workflows by employing adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) techniques to intercept and manipulate data, particularly using tools like evilginx2 for credential theft. They also use user execution tactics to trick victims into executing malicious code, followed by automated collection methods to harvest sensitive information.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries are leveraging the AiTM technique (T1557) to position themselves between users and legitimate services for data interception.
- Tools like evilginx2 are being used to steal credentials through phishing attacks that mimic trusted business workflows.
- User execution techniques (T1204) are employed to deceive victims into running malicious code, often via social engineering tactics.
- Automated collection methods (T1119) are utilized for the systematic harvesting of internal data once an adversary gains access to a network or system.
- Adversaries target multi-factor authentication mechanisms (T1111) to bypass security measures and gain unauthorized access to sensitive systems and services.
- Phantom Stealer campaigns often involve a multi-stage approach, combining multiple MITRE ATT&CK techniques for comprehensive data exfiltration.
