This includes Windows Security Event IDs related to logon events, process creation events, and potential exploitation of services or protocols like RDP.

- event.id (90% populated): Identifies specific event IDs such as 4624 (Account Logon), 4778 (Security Policy Change), and 4768/4769 (User Account Control events) which can indicate lateral movement activities.
