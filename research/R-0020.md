## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
CLOSEDQUORUM is a Windows malware that leverages up to four AI models for decision-making, allowing it to autonomously choose among various malicious actions such as stealing credentials and crypto wallet data without direct command from an attacker's server.

### Key Findings
- The malware employs up to four AI models (including DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral, and Google Gemini) to vote on its next move.
- CLOSEDQUORUM targets Windows credentials, saved browser passwords, and crypto wallet data for theft.
- Indicators of compromise include specific MD5, SHA1, and SHA256 hashes of the malware files.
- Cisco Talos has not observed a fully functional instance of CLOSEDQUORUM in the wild.
