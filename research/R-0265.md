## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries abuse the XCSSET malware by replacing legitimate browser binaries to capture credentials and monitor web traffic, enabling them to maintain persistence and exfiltrate sensitive information without detection. This technique typically involves sophisticated credential theft mechanisms to ensure continuous access.

### Key Findings
- XCSSET employs a malicious browser application that replaces genuine browser executables to intercept and monitor network traffic.
- The malware captures credentials and monitors web activities, allowing adversaries to steal login details and other sensitive data.
- Adversaries leverage code signing bypass techniques or use compromised developer environments to inject their malicious payloads into legitimate binaries.
- Use of multifactor authentication (MFA) significantly reduces the risk of credential theft by preventing unauthorized access even if credentials are intercepted.
- Network monitoring and anomaly detection can help identify AiTM attacks that modify traffic in real-time, which could be indicative of XCSSET activity.

### Key Fields
