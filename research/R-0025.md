## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Chinese threat actor UTA0565 exploited simultaneous Chrome-to-Windows zero-day vulnerabilities to deploy the CLEANGULP malware, highlighting advanced evasion techniques that manipulate process creation parameters to avoid detection.

### Key Findings
- UTA0565 used a combination of multiple zero-day exploits in both Google Chrome and Microsoft Windows to facilitate their attack.
- The deployment of CLEANGULP malware involved the use of fake websites as an initial infection vector.
- Attackers manipulated process creation parameters to evade detection by security solutions, showcasing sophisticated evasion tactics.
- Advanced Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) configurations and behavioral analytics are crucial for detecting such attacks.
