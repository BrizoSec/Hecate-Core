## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries often use adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) techniques to intercept and manipulate network traffic, particularly targeting children's popular games like Roblox and Minecraft. By positioning themselves between the victim and the server, attackers can steal data or credentials by employing methods such as ARP poisoning and proxy usage.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries employ AiTM techniques to position themselves between a user’s device and an online service, enabling them to capture sensitive information like login credentials.
- Common methods used include ARP spoofing and DNS hijacking to redirect traffic through malicious proxies or routers controlled by attackers.
- Tools like Wireshark and tcpdump are misused for network sniffing activities, aiding in the collection of intercepted data.
- Indicators of such attacks may include unusual network slowdowns, unexpected pop-ups asking for credentials on seemingly legitimate sites, and sudden disconnections from online services.
