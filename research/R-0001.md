## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
On August 5, 2026, adversaries utilized botnet-related techniques to distribute PureRAT and Overlord RAT payloads. The attacks involved the use of domains for command-and-control (C2) communication without specific details about active exploitation or vulnerabilities.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries used domains as part of their botnet infrastructure for C2 communication.
- PureRAT and Overlord RAT malware payloads were identified in the attacks, highlighting the use of remote access trojans.
- No specific vulnerabilities or active exploits were detailed, indicating that social engineering or other means might have been employed to distribute these payloads.
- Botnet activities suggest a broader campaign involving compromised systems for further malicious operations.
