## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries abuse the pass-the-hash technique by exploiting NTLM (NT LAN Manager) hashes to gain unauthorized access to systems without needing to know the actual password. This method allows attackers to move laterally within a network and maintain persistence.

### Key Findings
- Pass-the-Hash attacks are used to leverage NTLM authentication vulnerabilities.
- The attack method involves lateral movement and credential harvesting.
- Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) include unusual network traffic patterns, failed login attempts using NTLM hashes, and unauthorized access attempts.
- Tools such as Mimikatz can be used by attackers to extract NTLM hashes from memory or disk.
- The threat level is medium, suggesting active but not highly sophisticated exploitation.
- There's a potential link to vulnerabilities like ProxyShell that could aid in the execution of pass-the-hash attacks.
