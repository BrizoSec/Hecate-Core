## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
The AI supply chain involves the distribution and integration of code libraries, data sets, and machine learning models that form the foundational components of AI applications. Traditional security measures often fall short in detecting sophisticated threats such as SANDWORM_MODE, which embeds malicious code within these components to compromise the integrity of AI systems post-installation.

### Key Findings
- Component selection phase involves attackers publishing backdoored or poisoned artifacts to public repositories, bypassing initial security controls.
- AI supply chain attacks can leverage typosquatting techniques to trick developers and automated tools into using malicious packages.
- Traditional software composition analysis (SCA) methods may not effectively map out the complex dependencies in AI systems due to the unique nature of ML frameworks.
