## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
ScreenConnect RMM and Cloudflare Tunnels are legitimate technologies designed for remote management, monitoring, and secure internet access respectively. However, threat actors exploit these tools by using ScreenConnect to gain stealthy unauthorized access to networks and deploy malware via phishing campaigns, while Cloudflare Tunnels provide a veil of anonymity and temporary domain names for malicious activities.

### Key Findings
- Threat actors leverage the trusted reputation of ScreenConnect RMM to conduct undetected intrusions within corporate environments.
- Cloudflare Tunnels are abused to rapidly set up obfuscated phishing pages or malicious services that can bypass traditional detection methods due to their transient nature and use of legitimate-looking domain names.
- ScreenConnect-signed malware is distributed through trusted software lures, exploiting user trust in recognized brand signatures.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries abuse ScreenConnect Remote Management and Monitoring (RMM) tools along with Cloudflare Tunnels to deliver malware stealthily. They create temporary, low-cost infrastructure that allows them to rapidly deploy and remove malicious payloads while evading detection through trusted software lures and phishing tactics.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries use ScreenConnect RMM for lateral movement within compromised networks and deploying malware under the guise of legitimate remote management activities.
- Cloudflare Tunnels are exploited by threat actors to create ephemeral, secure connections that help them bypass network security measures and deliver Python-based or other executable malware without leaving a significant digital footprint.
- Threat actors frequently employ trusted software lures such as Microsoft Office documents or installers masquerading as well-known applications like Adobe Reader to distribute malicious payloads.
- The rapid deployment and dismantling of attack infrastructure through Cloudflare Tunnels enable threat actors to avoid detection by security systems, making these attacks harder to trace back to their origin.
