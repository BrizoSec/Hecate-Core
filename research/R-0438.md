### Summary

### Key Findings

- The breach resulted in significant operational disruptions and exposed millions of user records, indicating a severe vulnerability in the system’s data protection measures.
- Authorities have advised users to avoid using the Flexi Parking app and seek alternative payment methods due to security concerns.
