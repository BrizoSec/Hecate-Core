## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
In Q2 2026, the email threat landscape saw a notable decline in phishing activities following the disruption of the Tycoon2FA platform. However, there was an observed increase in social engineering attacks leveraging Microsoft Teams and other communication platforms for credential theft.

### Key Findings
- The takedown of the Tycoon2FA platform led to significant reductions in traditional phishing tactics.
- There is a marked rise in the use of Microsoft Teams for conducting vishing (voice phishing) and related social engineering attacks.
- Credential theft remains the primary objective of most phishing campaigns, with attackers adapting their methods to circumvent multifactor authentication and passkeys.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries in Q2 2026 abuse the T1027 technique by obfuscating malicious payloads delivered via Microsoft Teams and other trusted platforms, enabling them to bypass multi-factor authentication and evade detection. This includes using AiTM attacks that leverage calendar invite phishing tactics.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries are increasingly leveraging obfuscated files or information techniques to deliver malware through compressed, archived, or encrypted payloads in emails and Microsoft Teams messages.
- AiTM (Adversary-in-the-Middle) attacks have become a prevalent method for bypassing MFA mechanisms, allowing attackers to intercept and manipulate secure communications.
- Calendar invite phishing remains a critical threat vector, with adversaries tricking victims into clicking on obfuscated links or attachments within seemingly legitimate calendar invitations.
- Despite declines in traditional phishing campaigns due to the disruption of Tycoon2FA, sophisticated attacks through Microsoft Teams have surged, highlighting a shift towards more integrated and less conspicuous attack vectors.

In Q2 2026, the email threat landscape saw a decline in traditional phishing activities due to the disruption of Tycoon2FA but witnessed an increase in social engineering attacks through Microsoft Teams and other communication platforms. Adversaries have adapted by using obfuscated files or information techniques to deliver malware and bypass multi-factor authentication via AiTM attacks.

### Recommended Hypothesis
