## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
A typical spambot component within malware such as Astaroth is designed to harvest email addresses from compromised systems or networks and send out large volumes of spam emails. These bots often include features for command-and-control communication, which allows the attackers to manage their botnet remotely.

### Key Findings

- [UNCERTAIN] Newer spambot components may incorporate evasion techniques or exploit recent vulnerabilities not yet widely documented.

### Summary

To detect this activity, focus on network telemetry that captures outbound SMTP traffic, DNS requests for suspicious domains, and unusual SMB or HTTP/HTTPS activities indicative of C2 communications.

- process.network.connection (75% populated): Provides details about network connections initiated by processes on endpoints. This is essential for detecting unusual outbound connections that might be part of C2 or exfiltration activities.
