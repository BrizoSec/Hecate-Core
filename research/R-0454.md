## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are increasingly exploiting AI technologies for reconnaissance and social engineering purposes, leveraging the capabilities of AI to enhance their attack vectors. CrowdStrike's participation in the Open Secure AI Alliance aims to address these threats by advancing the security and safety standards of AI systems.

### Key Findings
- Prompt injection techniques enable adversaries to manipulate AI-driven chatbots and language models for malicious intent, such as extracting sensitive information or executing commands.
- Adversaries use AI tools to automate reconnaissance activities, identifying vulnerabilities and weaknesses in target networks more efficiently than traditional methods.
- Social engineering campaigns are enhanced through the use of AI-generated content that appears more authentic and convincing to victims.
- AI systems acting as privileged identities pose significant risks if compromised or misused by attackers for lateral movement within a network.
- The proliferation of cross-domain attacks highlights the need for unified defense strategies that incorporate AI security measures across different environments.
