## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
During tax season, cybercriminals exploit the stress and urgency of taxpayers to launch phishing and malware campaigns by impersonating official entities. These attacks often involve social engineering tactics designed to trick victims into disclosing sensitive financial or identity information.

### Key Findings
- Cybercriminals use social engineering techniques such as email spoofing and website cloning to appear legitimate, thereby deceiving users into providing personal data.
- Phishing campaigns are highly targeted towards Indian taxpayers who are likely under pressure to file their tax returns accurately and on time.
- The compromised information includes sensitive financial details and identity credentials, which can be used for further fraudulent activities.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries targeting Indian taxpayers during tax season often use phishing emails to deploy sophisticated malware, including RATs and banking trojans. These campaigns frequently employ the T1012 Query Registry technique to gather system information and evade detection.

### Key Findings
- Phishing campaigns impersonate India's Income Tax Department to deliver Gh0st RAT, AsyncRAT, and Blackmoon malware via malicious emails or documents.
- Adversaries leverage legitimate services like Alibaba Cloud for payload hosting and ipwho.is for geolocation to avoid detection and maintain persistence.
- Malware often uses the Query Registry technique (T1012) to gather system information and evade security measures.
- Attackers utilize multi-stage techniques, deploying different malware strains through separate Command and Control channels.
