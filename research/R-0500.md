## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
GoldDigger is an advanced Android banking malware that leverages Virbox Protector for evasion and obfuscation, hooks the libart.so library to manipulate bytecode at runtime, and uses Accessibility Services to intercept and steal sensitive user information such as banking credentials and SMS messages.

### Key Findings
- GoldDigger utilizes Virbox Protector to evade detection by security software and modify its behavior dynamically.
- The malware abuses Android's Accessibility Service to gain unauthorized access to users' personal data, including banking credentials and SMS messages.
- By hooking the libart.so library, GoldDigger can intercept class loading and manipulate bytecode at runtime, allowing it to hide malicious activities from detection.
- GoldDigger streams stolen sensitive information to command-and-control servers, indicating a sophisticated C2 communication infrastructure.
