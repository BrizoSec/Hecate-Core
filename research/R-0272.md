## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are leveraging Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP) by using Valid Accounts to gain interactive sessions on remote systems, allowing them to perform actions as the logged-on user. This technique is part of a broader trend where adversaries evolve their tactics with AI-driven tools to bypass and adapt to stronger cyber defenses.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries are increasingly using RDP with Valid Accounts to maintain persistence in compromised environments.
- AI technologies enable adversaries to dynamically adjust their attack strategies, including the use of RDP, to evade detection by security systems.
- The evolving nature of adversary techniques suggests a need for continuous monitoring and adaptive defense mechanisms that can detect RDP abuse in real-time.
