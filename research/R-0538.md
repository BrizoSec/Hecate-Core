## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries may exploit vulnerabilities introduced by new software releases to gain unauthorized access, escalate privileges, or exfiltrate data. They could also leverage misconfigurations and weaknesses in the updated system to execute attacks more efficiently.

### Key Findings
- Attackers might use phishing emails or social engineering tactics to trick users into installing malicious versions of updates or granting unnecessary permissions.
- [UNCERTAIN] Adversaries could exploit zero-day vulnerabilities in newly released features if patches are not immediately available, allowing them to establish a foothold before defenses are strengthened.
- Adversaries often search for misconfigured cloud resources that allow public access without proper authentication mechanisms. This can be exacerbated by rushed deployments following major updates.
- Attackers frequently employ reconnaissance techniques such as querying metadata APIs and scanning IP ranges associated with newly released services to gather information about the environment before launching an attack.
