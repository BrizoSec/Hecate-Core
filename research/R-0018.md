## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries frequently employ credential theft as a primary method to gain unauthorized access, exploiting zero-trust environments where they may still find vulnerabilities or misconfigurations. CrowdStrike's Falcon Secure Access and Agentic SOC strategies are designed to mitigate such risks by implementing advanced detection mechanisms and proactive defense measures.

### Key Findings
- Credential theft remains a favored tactic among adversaries due to its effectiveness in gaining unauthorized access to systems.
- Adversaries leverage social engineering techniques alongside automated tools to steal credentials efficiently.
- Zero-trust environments, while secure, can still have misconfigurations that allow for credential abuse if not properly managed and monitored.
- CrowdStrike's ATT&CK framework aids in understanding adversary tactics and improving defensive strategies against credential theft.
- Falcon Secure Access enhances browser security by enforcing zero trust principles to prevent malware-free intrusions and unauthorized access.
- Agentic SOC leverages AI-driven technologies to detect and respond to threats proactively, thereby reducing the impact of successful credential theft.
