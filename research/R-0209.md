## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
According to Talos Intelligence's Threat Source newsletter, attackers succeed in cyberattacks approximately 54% of the time, highlighting the persistent challenge defenders face. This statistic underscores the critical importance of proactive security measures such as prioritized patching and continuous threat intelligence.

### Key Findings
- Attackers frequently exploit known vulnerabilities through targeted campaigns, emphasizing the need for rapid patch management.
- The myth that attackers only need one successful attempt while defenders must succeed all the time is challenged by the data showing a 54% success rate for attackers.
- Smart and prioritized patching strategies are becoming increasingly important as cyber threats evolve rapidly.
- Threat actors utilize various tactics, including social engineering and malware distribution via legitimate services like PDFium in Google Chrome.

## 3. Telemetry Mapping: OCSF Fields

### Summary
To detect behaviors indicative of an adversary 'winning 54% of the time', telemetry should focus on identifying repeated attempts to compromise or move laterally within the network, particularly through common attack vectors like credential abuse and remote service exploitation.

### Key Fields
- process_creation (80% populated): Tracks when a new process is created on endpoints, which can indicate malicious binaries executing.
- network_connection: Identifies established connections to external IP addresses or known malicious domains, indicating potential exfiltration attempts or lateral movement.
- file_change: Monitors changes in files such as modification times and access rights that might signal tampering with system files for privilege escalation.
- credential_use (50% populated): Detects when credentials are used by processes, especially if they do not match expected patterns of user behavior, suggesting credential reuse or abuse.
