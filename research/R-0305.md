### Summary
The AI supply chain involves transforming raw data into live, production-ready AI systems through various stages including data collection, model training, and deployment. ## 5. Research Synthesis

### Executive Summary
SANDWORM_MODE represents a sophisticated supply chain attack where adversaries exploit trust in AI toolchains to inject malicious code, leading to data breaches and widespread compromise. Effective detection requires monitoring for unusual patterns in prompt generation, network communications with suspicious IPs, and deviations from baseline behavior.

### Recommended Hypothesis
