## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are leveraging advanced AI technologies to rapidly identify and exploit vulnerabilities in systems, thereby collapsing the window of opportunity for defenders to respond. CrowdStrike highlights the importance of collaboration between cybersecurity companies and AI developers like OpenAI to develop robust defense strategies.

### Key Findings
- Frontier AI is enabling adversaries to quickly discover and deploy zero-day exploits before security patches are available.
- CrowdStrike observes that weaponized documents in spear phishing campaigns remain a popular technique for exploiting system vulnerabilities.
- The integration of AI into cyber defense mechanisms necessitates continuous learning and adaptation by defenders to stay ahead of attackers.
- Defenders must adopt a 'trust but verify' approach when leveraging AI capabilities, ensuring robust validation processes are in place to mitigate potential risks.

## 3. Telemetry Mapping: OCSF Fields

### Summary
The behavior associated with leveraging frontier AI technologies for enhanced cybersecurity involves real-time detection and mitigation of threats by analyzing various types of logs and events. This includes monitoring user activities, system processes, network communications, and endpoint telemetry to identify anomalies indicative of malicious activities or suspicious patterns that deviate from the norm.

### Key Fields
- process_name (10% populated): Identifies specific executables launched on endpoints which may indicate unusual activity if they are uncommon or unexpected in a given context. This field is crucial for detecting potentially malicious software or scripts running on systems.
- network.destination.ip_address (5% populated): Tracks IP addresses where network traffic is destined, helping to identify connections to known malicious domains or IPs which could be indicative of data exfiltration or command and control communications.
- event_id (80% populated): Captures specific Windows Security Event IDs such as 4624 for account logon events, 4634 for a user's profile was created, and other relevant event IDs. These provide detailed insights into user activities that can help detect initial access vectors or lateral movement.
- event_data.process_id (50% populated): Indicates the process ID of processes running on endpoints which is vital in tracking the lifecycle of a potentially compromised system and understanding how malicious actors might escalate privileges or spread within the network.

## 5. Research Synthesis

### Recommended Hypothesis
