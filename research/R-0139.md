## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Mirage Kitten employs brute force attacks and network discovery techniques to target systems in the Middle East and Africa, leveraging tactics similar to those of known adversaries like Fox Kitten and APT34. These operations involve scanning for security software and probing system connections to identify potential vulnerabilities.

### Key Findings
- Mirage Kitten uses brute force attacks (MITRE ATT&CK T1110) to gain unauthorized access to targeted systems, often in conjunction with network discovery techniques (T1049).
- The adversary performs registry queries (T1012) on compromised hosts to gather information about the system and environment.
- Mirage Kitten is active across a wide geographical area including the Middle East and Africa, indicating sophisticated coordination and regional targeting strategies.
- Tools such as netstat are used by adversaries like Andariel for network connection discovery (T1049), showing similarities in tactics among different APT groups.
