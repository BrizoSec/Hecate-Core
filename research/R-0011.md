## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries often abuse credential theft techniques to gain unauthorized access to systems by stealing login credentials, which they then use for lateral movement and privilege escalation within a network. CrowdStrike's approach focuses on disrupting such tactics through advanced threat detection and response using AI-driven methodologies and the ATT&CK framework.

### Key Findings
- Credential theft is a favored tactic among adversaries due to its effectiveness in gaining unauthorized access.
- CrowdStrike utilizes AI-driven techniques to detect and respond to credential theft attacks, enhancing security operations centers' capabilities.
- The ATT&CK framework is employed by CrowdStrike to provide visibility into adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures for non-security stakeholders.
- Falcon Secure Access sets a standard for zero trust browser security, mitigating the risks associated with malware-free intrusions.
- CrowdStrike offers continuous updates for cloud security solutions like Falcon Cloud Security, including enhancements for Azure and Google Cloud environments.
