## 3. Telemetry Mapping: OCSF Fields

### Summary
To detect behaviors indicative of SANDWORM_MODE or similar AI toolchain supply chain attacks, focus on monitoring anomalous activities in the system that deviate from established baselines. Key areas include unusual file modifications, process creation with elevated privileges, network communications to suspicious endpoints, and signs of lateral movement within a network.

### Key Fields
- process_creation: Represents any new process creation event which can indicate malicious activity such as loading a payload or establishing command and control (C2) communication. High volume during non-standard working hours could be an indicator of compromise.
- file_modification: Tracks changes to system files, especially those related to security mechanisms like antivirus definitions or firewall rules. Sudden modifications without known legitimate reasons may suggest tampering by malicious actors.
- network_communication: Captures outbound and inbound network connections which can reveal suspicious communication patterns indicative of data exfiltration attempts or C2 activity. Monitoring DNS requests and HTTP/HTTPS traffic for unusual endpoints is crucial.
- credential_usage: Logs the use of credentials, particularly those with elevated privileges. Unauthorized access or unexpected usage patterns (e.g., during off-hours) could point to credential theft or misuse by attackers.
- lateral_movement: Identifies activities that suggest an attacker moving laterally within a network, such as RDP connections, SMB shares access, or WMI queries from unknown sources. This is indicative of an attempt to expand control over the internal network.
