This method is particularly effective for those with privileged access who can leverage it to maintain persistence and control over targeted networks.

### Key Findings

- Tools such as SSLstrip, ARP spoofing tools, and DNS poisoning utilities are commonly used to perform AiTM attacks.
- Indicators of Compromise (IoCs) include unexpected changes in network behavior, unauthorized access patterns, and anomalies in encrypted traffic.
- Detection of AiTM requires monitoring for unusual network events, such as certificate warnings or unexpected redirections.
