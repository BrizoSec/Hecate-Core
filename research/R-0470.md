## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Project CAV3RN leverages Google Apps Script to establish a stealthy command-and-control (C2) channel for cyberespionage against Israeli organizations, blending malicious traffic with legitimate activity to evade detection and maintain operational security.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries are using sophisticated modular frameworks like Project CAV3RN to target specific regions, such as Israel, indicating a high level of targeting and resource allocation.
- Google Apps Script is being abused for bidirectional communication between compromised systems and the attackers' infrastructure, allowing for data exfiltration while mimicking normal web traffic patterns.
- The attack involves redirecting intercepted personal data through Google's servers to an Israel-based domain (analit.tech), highlighting the use of legitimate services to conceal malicious activities.
- This technique demonstrates that threat actors are adopting more complex and dynamic C2 methodologies, such as modular frameworks and cloud service abuse, to enhance their operational security and evade traditional detection mechanisms.
