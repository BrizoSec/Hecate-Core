## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries exploit AI systems through prompt injection techniques to manipulate or extract sensitive information, undermining the integrity and confidentiality of these systems. CrowdStrike's new Prompt Injection Taxonomy offers a detailed framework for understanding how such attacks are executed and concealed.

### Key Findings
- Prompt injection attacks can be used to alter the behavior of AI models by injecting malicious prompts that result in unexpected outputs or actions, thereby compromising system security.
- Adversaries leverage the complexity and opacity of AI systems to hide their activities, making detection challenging for traditional security measures.
- Security teams need a comprehensive taxonomy like CrowdStrike's Prompt Injection Taxonomy to better understand, detect, and mitigate prompt injection attacks effectively.
- The taxonomy provides insights into how attackers can manipulate inputs to influence AI decisions or outputs in unintended ways, highlighting the importance of robust input validation and monitoring.
- Developers and engineers must incorporate security best practices during AI system design and deployment to prevent exploitation through prompt injection.
