### Key Fields
- event_id (100% populated): Event IDs such as 4624 (account login) provide critical information on user activity which is essential for identifying unauthorized access attempts or lateral movement.

- network_connection (80% populated): Information about outbound network connections can help in identifying C2 communications and data exfiltration attempts.
