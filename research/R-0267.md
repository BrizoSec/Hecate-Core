## 3. Telemetry Mapping: OCSF Fields

### Summary
To detect AI-driven attacks using automation tools, we need to capture telemetry that includes detailed network flow information, authentication logs for both success and failure events, process execution details, and network communications data such as HTTP requests and DNS queries.

### Key Fields
- network.flow (highly populated): Captures the direction, protocol, and volume of network traffic which helps in identifying scanning activities or unusual lateral movement patterns.
- event.auth.success & event.auth.failure (moderately to highly populated): Provides insight into authentication attempts, aiding in the detection of brute-force attacks and credential misuse.
- process.execution (highly populated): Tracks process details including parent-child relationships crucial for identifying suspicious processes such as those involved in lateral movement or establishing C2 communications.
- network.http.request & network.dns.query (moderately to highly populated): Essential for capturing HTTP requests and DNS queries, which are often used by attackers to communicate with their command-and-control servers.
