## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries exploit AI systems through techniques like prompt injection to manipulate or coerce responses from AI models, often for malicious purposes such as spreading disinformation or gaining unauthorized access. CrowdStrike's participation in the Open Secure AI Alliance aims to develop and implement security measures against these threats.

### Key Findings
- Prompt injection allows adversaries to bypass input sanitization mechanisms by crafting inputs that manipulate AI responses.
- Adversaries use social engineering tactics to identify and exploit vulnerabilities within AI systems, targeting both developers and end-users.
- CrowdStrike's Falcon AIDR detects and mitigates prompt injection attacks in real-time, enhancing the security posture of organizations against AI-based threats.
- Indicators of compromise include unexpected or inappropriate responses from AI models, unusual patterns of API requests, and deviations from baseline model behavior.
