## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
SANDWORM_MODE is a sophisticated self-propagating worm that leverages OIDC tokens to spread across npm namespaces, uses an eBPF rootkit for kernel-level evasion, and employs UPX and custom encryption to avoid detection by signature-based unpackers. Detection involves monitoring CI runners and token exchanges.

### Key Findings
- SANDWORM_MODE utilizes OIDC tokens for republishing itself in npm namespaces.
- The worm employs an eBPF rootkit to hide its presence at the kernel level.
- Custom encryption and UPX packing are used to evade signature-based detection methods.
