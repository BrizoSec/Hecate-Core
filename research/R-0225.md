## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries in the recent cyber attack on Cisco employed sophisticated tactics such as privilege escalation, credential dumping, and lateral movement to establish a foothold within the network. They used a CloudZ remote access tool (RAT) and an undocumented plugin called “Pheno” to maintain persistence and evade detection.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries linked to UNC2447 and Lapsus$ deployed a RAT named CloudZ, along with a previously undocumented plugin 'Pheno,' to conduct lateral movement and establish persistence within the network.
- The attack involved credential dumping and privilege escalation techniques to gain access to enterprise-level domain administrator credentials.
- Talos Incident Response associates pre-ransomware activity with attempts by threat actors to obtain domain administrator privileges, emphasizing the need for robust detection mechanisms against such attacks.
- Adversaries hijacked old email threads from third-party organizations’ Exchange servers or individual inboxes to gather information relevant to their target.
