## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries with privileged network access leverage adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) attacks to intercept and manipulate real-time network traffic, enabling them to perform activities such as data exfiltration, credential theft, and other malicious actions.

### Key Findings
- ARP poisoning is a common method used in AiTM attacks to redirect traffic through the attacker's machine.
- Adversaries often use proxy servers or man-in-the-middle (MITM) tools to intercept HTTPS traffic by deploying rogue certificates.
- Monitoring for unexpected network anomalies, such as unusual DNS requests or TCP SYN flood patterns, can indicate ongoing AiTM activities.
- Detection of unauthorized access to ARP tables and SSL/TLS certificate discrepancies are critical indicators of AiTM attacks.
