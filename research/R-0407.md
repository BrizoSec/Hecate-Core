## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries may abuse the lack of transparency and shared research in AI security by exploiting vulnerabilities in closed or proprietary AI systems, which can hinder defenders' ability to understand and mitigate new attack vectors. CrowdStrike's involvement in the Open Secure AI Alliance highlights the importance of open models and collaborative research to enhance detection and defense against such threats.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries exploit newly discovered vulnerabilities in software patches like those released by Microsoft, leveraging zero-day exploits before they are mitigated.
- Prompt injection techniques allow attackers to manipulate input data for AI systems, leading to unintended outputs or system misbehaviors that can be used to bypass security measures.
- The use of Falcon Exposure Management and other advanced tools by CrowdStrike enables organizations to better understand their third-party risk exposure and improve their cybersecurity posture.
- AI-driven data loss prevention mechanisms are targeted by sophisticated adversaries who seek to exfiltrate sensitive information using techniques that circumvent traditional detection methods.
