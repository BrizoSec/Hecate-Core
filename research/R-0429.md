## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries abuse AI-related vulnerabilities by exploiting prompt injection techniques to manipulate or control AI systems, leading to unauthorized actions such as data theft, system disruption, and the deployment of malware. CrowdStrike's involvement in the Open Secure AI Alliance aims to mitigate these threats through collective research and open-source tools.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries exploit prompt injection techniques to manipulate AI-driven security systems and gain unauthorized access.
- Prompt injection attacks are used to bypass traditional security measures by leveraging weaknesses in natural language processing (NLP) models.
- CrowdStrike’s Prompt Injection Taxonomy provides a comprehensive guide for detecting and mitigating such attacks, highlighting the importance of continuous monitoring and adaptive defense strategies.
- The use of open-source AI models and shared tools within the Open Secure AI Alliance enhances collaborative efforts to develop more robust security measures against emerging threats.
