## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
A suspected Chinese-speaking threat actor, operating as Red Heron, exploited multiple vulnerabilities in WordPress and ZyXEL switches from May to September 2026. The attacker used these exploits to steal over 18,000 sensitive records from a western government organization by compromising WordPress installations with CVE-2026-63030 and CVE-2026-60137.

### Key Findings
- The adversary exploited WordPress vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-63030, CVE-2026-60137) to breach a government organization's systems, resulting in the theft of over 18,000 sensitive records.
- The attacker also compromised ZyXEL GS1900 switches using CVE-2026-7273, affecting 996 devices across 48 countries and exfiltrating configurations and credentials.
- Custom tool development was observed by the threat actor, possibly leveraging large language models for advanced capabilities such as privilege escalation and systematic data collection.
- The attacker maintained persistence using a single IP address (104.225.153.141) to target multiple technologies and exploit various CVEs.
