## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
The DarkSword exploit chain leverages zero-day or unpatched vulnerabilities in Safari to execute code purely through page rendering, bypassing the need for user interaction. It deploys a payload on vulnerable iOS devices designed to exfiltrate sensitive local data such as credentials and cryptocurrency wallets while establishing persistent communication with remote command-and-control infrastructure.

### Key Findings
- The attack vector relies on a fake 'iPhone Duo preorder' landing page that mimics legitimate Apple design, triggering the exploit automatically upon opening in Safari without requiring clicks or downloads.
- The malware targets iOS versions prior to the patch released by Apple (referenced as patched for vulnerabilities disclosed in March 2026), indicating reliance on specific unpatched kernel or webkit flaws.
- Two distinct Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) are identified: 'pnmrud.cc' functions as the command-and-control and data collection server, while 'cloud.cmatgldn.click' acts as an ad click and conversion tracker to monitor campaign effectiveness.
- The post-exploitation payload actively exfiltrates high-value personal data including saved credentials, cryptocurrency wallet keys, notes, messages, and contacts, while attempting to evade detection by deleting local diagnostic reports.
