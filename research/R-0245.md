## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are abusing the pass-the-hash (PtH) attack technique by leveraging stolen hashed credentials to create new sessions within Krawczyk Industries Limited's network, thereby bypassing the need for original passwords and enabling unauthorized access with medium-level threat intensity.

### Key Findings
- Pass-the-Hash attacks involve stealing hashed credentials from compromised systems or accounts to impersonate legitimate users without needing their password.
- Adversaries are using this technique to escalate privileges within the network, allowing them to move laterally and gain control over additional resources.
- The attack is categorized with a medium threat level indicating significant potential for damage but not immediate critical danger.
- Indicators of compromise (IOCs) related to PtH attacks include unusual authentication attempts from unexpected locations or times and use of specific tools designed to exploit hashed credentials.
- Organizations should implement robust multifactor authentication (MFA) and monitor execution points for signs of such attack vectors.
