## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries may abuse the CrowdStrike Falcon Platform's dynamic risk assessment capabilities by exploiting vulnerabilities prioritized before full mitigations are applied, or by targeting less critical but more accessible assets to evade detection. They could also manipulate data inputs related to asset context and threat intelligence to lower their perceived risk score.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries exploit the window between vulnerability disclosure and patch deployment through targeted attacks on prioritized security updates.
- Attackers may focus on non-critical assets with less stringent monitoring, thereby avoiding high-risk categories that attract immediate attention from threat detection systems.
- Manipulation of contextual data such as asset criticality or attack path analysis can artificially reduce the perceived risk score and prolong the dwell time within a network undetected.
- CrowdStrike's advanced techniques for identifying and mitigating threats are challenged by sophisticated adversaries who employ tactics to bypass these mechanisms, requiring continuous updates and improvements.

## 3. Telemetry Mapping: OCSF Fields

### Summary
Telemetry capturing the behavior of ransomware attacks would involve monitoring endpoint-based events such as file modifications (write/overwrite/delete), process creations (especially those involving encryption tools or scripts), network communications that may indicate lateral movement, and user activities like credential dumping. This data helps in identifying suspicious patterns characteristic of ransomware attack stages.

### Key Fields
- process_creation (90% populated): Tracks the creation of new processes on endpoints, which is critical for detecting ransomware execution as it typically involves running malicious binaries or scripts.
- file_modification (85% populated): Logs file write/overwrite operations that ransomware uses to encrypt files; this includes timestamps and access paths of affected files.
- network_connection (70% populated): Monitors outbound network connections made by endpoints, useful for identifying command-and-control communications or data exfiltration attempts.
- credential_dump (50% populated): Detects unauthorized access to credential stores, often a precursor to lateral movement or privilege escalation in ransomware attacks.
