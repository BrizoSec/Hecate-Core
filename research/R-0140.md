## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
The European Union Customs Union (EUCU) applies a set of standardized customs charges and duties to imports from outside the EU based on their CIF value, which includes cost, insurance, and freight. These charges are designed to harmonize trade practices among member states and certain non-member territories within the EUCU.

### Key Findings
- Customs charges for imports outside the EU are determined by a standardized system that calculates costs including duty based on CIF value.
- The European Union Customs Union applies these regulations to all its member states, Monaco, and British Overseas Territory of Akrotiri and Dhekelia.
- The specific workings of customs duties and taxes may vary but generally involve calculating charges based on the CIF value for import orders.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries exploit the Adversary-in-the-Middle (AiTM) technique to intercept and manipulate communications between online order systems and customers, potentially altering data and charges. This allows them to access sensitive information by abusing permissions and exploiting vulnerabilities in input validation.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries use AiTM attacks to block or spoof reporting messages and modify parameters within the communication channels.
- Unauthorized command messages can be injected into systems to perform malicious actions, such as altering order data and charges.
- Multiple applications are vulnerable due to improper permission management and input validation flaws.
- Credential theft via phishing, brute force, or credential dumping is commonly observed by CrowdStrike in conjunction with AiTM attacks.
- Adversaries leverage the permissions granted to apps on mobile devices to access sensitive files and data.
