## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are exploiting the Business Account Manager service offered by Meta to discover and enumerate cloud resources, which they can then use for further malicious activities. This technique involves identifying cloud services post-access to gather information on available assets that could be targeted.

### Key Findings
- Meta's Business Account Manager is being abused as a reconnaissance tool by adversaries to identify potential targets within an organization's cloud infrastructure.
- The attackers are using the service to enumerate cloud resources such as IaaS and PaaS environments, which aids in planning subsequent attacks.
- This method allows threat actors to gain valuable insights into the structure of cloud services, including AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud accounts linked through Meta Business Manager.
- Indicators of compromise may include unusual access patterns from Meta's Business Account Manager interface and unexpected enumeration activities on cloud service consoles.
