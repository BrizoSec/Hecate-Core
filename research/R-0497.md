## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries exploit the BabaDeda chain to deliver CNCMachineRMS, a RAT that provides persistent remote access through legitimate IBM SPSS IDE. The attack starts with ClickFix social engineering and progresses through multi-stage payloads delivered in memory.

### Key Findings
- CNCMachineRMS is deployed after a multistage BabaDeda delivery chain involving ClickFix social engineering.
- The RAT uses obfuscation techniques to evade detection, including inserting shellcode into random noise within allocated buffers.
- Persistence mechanisms are established by placing CNCMachineRMS files in `%TEMP%` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%`, mimicking legitimate SPSS IDE behavior.
- Detection focuses on identifying unusual activity with IBM SPSS IDE and specific file indicators related to the RAT's persistence methods.

## 5. Research Synthesis

### Executive Summary
CNCMachineRMS is an undocumented RAT that infiltrates industrial control systems, particularly CNC machines, through a multistage BabaDeda chain exploiting ClickFix social engineering. It uses obfuscation techniques and mimics legitimate SPSS IDE behavior to establish persistence, posing significant risks to critical infrastructure security.

### Recommended Hypothesis
> Adversaries use multi-stage ClickFix social engineering campaigns to deploy CNCMachineRMS for persistent remote access on industrial control systems.

### Gaps Identified
- There is a lack of comprehensive detection mechanisms targeting the initial stages of the BabaDeda chain, focusing mainly on post-compromise activities.
- Current coverage does not adequately address the encryption and data integrity measures in place within RMS systems, potentially missing indicators of compromised resources.

### Key Findings
- Hypothesis: Adversaries use multi-stage ClickFix social engineering campaigns to deploy CNCMachineRMS for persistent remote access on industrial control systems.
- Gap: There is a lack of comprehensive detection mechanisms targeting the initial stages of the BabaDeda chain, focusing mainly on post-compromise activities.
- Gap: Current coverage does not adequately address the encryption and data integrity measures in place within RMS systems, potentially missing indicators of compromised resources.
- Focus: Enhance monitoring for social engineering attempts related to ClickFix and other similar services to identify early stages of intrusion.
- Focus: Develop more granular detection rules for process creation events involving IBM SPSS IDE and related binaries to catch CNCMachineRMS activities.
