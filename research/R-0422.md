## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries like Dysphoria exploit fallback channels (T1008) to maintain reliable command and control by using secondary communication methods if the primary channel is disrupted or monitored. This technique is critical for botnet operations, enabling lateral movement across networks without detection.

### Key Findings
- Botnets use fallback channels to ensure continuous communication between C2 servers and infected devices even when traditional channels are compromised or blocked.
- Adversaries implement redundant communication protocols such as DNS tunneling or HTTP requests disguised in normal traffic patterns to evade network security measures.
- Indicators of compromise include unusual outbound traffic from unexpected ports, high volumes of data exchanged via non-standard protocols, and the presence of encrypted communications with no apparent purpose.
- Detection of fallback channels requires a comprehensive monitoring strategy that includes deep packet inspection and behavioral analysis to identify anomalous communication patterns.
