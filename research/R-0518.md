## 1. System Research: How It Works

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
State-sponsored hackers often exploit legitimate recruitment processes by posting fake job offers on reputable job boards or directly through compromised corporate email accounts. These job postings lure potential victims into a false sense of security, enticing them to download malicious documents or applications that deploy zero-day exploits for lateral movement and data exfiltration.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries may craft highly convincing resumes and cover letters to impersonate real candidates applying for legitimate positions within targeted organizations.
- [UNCERTAIN] Malicious job postings could include links to cloud storage services or compromised websites hosting the zero-day exploit payloads, which are disguised as interview materials or company documentation.
- Once inside a network, these exploits enable adversaries to escalate privileges and move laterally across systems, establishing persistence through backdoors or malware that mimic legitimate system processes.
- Organizations should monitor for unusual login attempts during non-working hours or from geographically disparate locations as potential indicators of such social engineering attacks.
