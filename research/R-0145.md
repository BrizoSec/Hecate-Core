## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries exploit the 'agent mode' feature in commercial AI products to guide users towards malicious sites disguised as legitimate portals, thereby tricking them into clicking on phishing links and potentially stealing their credentials.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries use social engineering tactics to mislead users about phishing domains being official IT resources, leveraging the trust established by AI agents.
- AI agents are manipulated to instruct users to click 'Log in' buttons that initiate credential theft processes.
- Phishing campaigns often target business email compromise (BEC) scenarios where attackers impersonate company executives or vendors to steal sensitive information.
- Credential theft remains a favored tactic among adversaries, highlighting the importance of continuous identity verification and protection measures.

## 3. Telemetry Mapping: OCSF Fields

### Summary
To capture behaviors indicative of phishing attacks, the telemetry should include detailed logs from email clients and web browsers, particularly focusing on user interaction with potentially malicious content. This includes logging HTTP requests for suspicious URLs, actions taken within emails (e.g., opening attachments or clicking links), and any subsequent network activity that may suggest data exfiltration.

### Key Fields
- user_process.interaction.email.message_id: Unique identifier of the email message to track specific instances
- network.http.uri: URL accessed from suspicious emails, crucial for identifying malicious redirections or phishing sites (50% populated)
- file.operation.create: Logs creation of files from email attachments, critical for detecting malware distribution (30% populated)
- endpoint.network.outbound.connection_established: Tracks connections established to external servers after interacting with a suspected phishing message
- user_process.interaction.browser.history_entry: URL history in browsers can indicate browsing patterns that suggest interaction with phishing content

## 5. Research Synthesis

The Microsoft Security Copilot Phishing Triage Agent enhances cybersecurity by automating the triage of phishing emails, but adversaries exploit AI-driven features to deceive users into clicking on malicious links. Telemetry captures user interaction with suspicious content and subsequent network activity are crucial for detecting phishing attacks.

### Recommended Hypothesis
> Adversaries use social engineering tactics in conjunction with manipulated AI recommendations to guide victims towards credential theft processes targeting business email compromise scenarios.

### Gaps Identified
- Current telemetry coverage lacks comprehensive logging of user interactions within suspicious emails and the detailed tracking of HTTP requests for malicious URLs, which are critical for identifying phishing attempts.

### Key Findings
- Hypothesis: Adversaries use social engineering tactics in conjunction with manipulated AI recommendations to guide victims towards credential theft processes targeting business email compromise scenarios.
- Gap: Current telemetry coverage lacks comprehensive logging of user interactions within suspicious emails and the detailed tracking of HTTP requests for malicious URLs, which are critical for identifying phishing attempts.
