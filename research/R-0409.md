## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are exploiting AI systems through prompt injection techniques, which involve manipulating inputs to generate unintended or harmful outputs. CrowdStrike tracks over 180 different prompt injection methods and has joined the Open Secure AI Alliance to enhance AI security measures.

### Key Findings
- Prompt injection is a prevalent attack vector for adversaries aiming to exploit vulnerabilities in AI systems.
- CrowdStrike has identified more than 180 unique prompt injection techniques, highlighting the diversity of threats in this area.
- The new Prompt Injection Taxonomy provides security teams with detailed insights into how these attacks operate and hide, aiding in better detection and mitigation strategies.
- Joining the Open Secure AI Alliance allows CrowdStrike to collaborate on developing shared tools for testing AI systems against real-world adversary behaviors.
- Falcon AIDR is a key tool developed by CrowdStrike that helps secure the attack surface of AI systems by identifying potential vulnerabilities and threats.
