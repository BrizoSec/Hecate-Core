## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries may attempt to exploit vulnerabilities in the CrowdStrike Falcon Platform by targeting misconfigurations and unpatched systems, leveraging third-party EDR tools integration for lateral movement or data exfiltration. They could also manipulate IOAs (Indicator of Activity) within Falcon Next-Gen SIEM to evade detection or conduct reconnaissance.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries are likely to focus on identifying and exploiting misconfigurations in cloud environments managed by Falcon Exposure Management.
- Integration with third-party EDR tools, such as Microsoft Defender, can be leveraged for lateral movement within a network.
- IOAs (Indicators of Activity) within the Falcon Next-Gen SIEM system might be manipulated or abused to evade detection and conduct reconnaissance.
- Vulnerability management practices in CrowdStrike Falcon Platform are critical but can also introduce attack vectors if not properly secured.
- Data protection features in Falcon Data Security and DSPM may be targeted by sophisticated threats that seek to exploit runtime vulnerabilities for data exfiltration.
- The use of ISO 42001:2023 standards can provide a framework for assessing risks, but adversaries might find ways to circumvent these protections through social engineering or supply chain attacks.
