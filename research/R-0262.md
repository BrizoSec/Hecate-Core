## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries exploit AI agents by leveraging their identity and access management weaknesses to gain unauthorized access, often through credential theft or session hijacking. This allows them to inject malicious instructions into legitimate sessions for stealthy attacks without detection.

### Key Findings
- Credential theft is a common method used by adversaries to compromise AI agent identities.
- Session hijacking enables attackers to insert hidden instructions between client-server communications, facilitating covert operations.
- Identity and access management (IAM) deficiencies in AI agents provide entry points for evasive attacks.
- Adversaries may use continuous identity mechanisms against AI agents to track and exploit legitimate user activities.
