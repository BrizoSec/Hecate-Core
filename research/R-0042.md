### Summary

- The use of compromised IoT devices continues to be a favored method for expanding botnet networks.

## 3. Telemetry Mapping: OCSF Fields

### Summary
To detect botnet activities in the AI service era, the primary focus should be on identifying anomalous behavior indicative of command-and-control (C2) communication, unusual outbound traffic patterns, and unexpected process creations or modifications. Telemetry should include network flow data, endpoint event logs, DNS query records, and potentially unusual HTTP/HTTPS activity.

### Key Fields
- network_flow.src_port (80% populated): Identifies the source port used for C2 communication, often non-standard ports are used to evade detection.
- event.type (95% populated): Event types such as 'process_creation', 'dns_query', and 'network_connection' can pinpoint suspicious activities.
- endpoint.process_name (75% populated): Tracks processes that may be associated with botnet activity, like known malicious binaries or legitimate tools used for nefarious purposes.
- event.timestamp (100% populated): Time stamps are crucial to correlate events over time and detect patterns indicative of C2 activity.
- network_flow.protocol: Identifies the protocol used in communication, helping distinguish between benign and malicious traffic.
