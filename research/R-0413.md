## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries exploit AI systems through prompt injection techniques by manipulating input prompts to elicit unintended responses from the models, potentially leading to data leakage or system compromise. CrowdStrike tracks over 180 such methods and has joined the Open Secure AI Alliance to enhance security measures against these threats.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries utilize prompt injection as a technique to manipulate AI systems into performing unauthorized actions.
- CrowdStrike identifies more than 180 unique prompt injection methods, indicating the evolving nature of this threat vector.
- The Prompt Injection Taxonomy provides security teams with comprehensive guidance on understanding and mitigating these attacks.
- Joining the Open Secure AI Alliance allows CrowdStrike to collaborate on developing safer and more secure AI systems through shared knowledge and tools.
- Falcon AIDR, an advanced detection system by CrowdStrike, helps in securing the attack surface of AI models against prompt injection and other threats.
- The importance of open-source models and collaborative testing frameworks is highlighted for identifying vulnerabilities before they can be exploited.
