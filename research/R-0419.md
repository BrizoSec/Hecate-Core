### Key Fields
- user_id (90% populated): Unique identifier for each user accessing systems; helps track individual user activity patterns over time.
- event_time (100% populated): Timestamp of when an event occurred, crucial for identifying abnormal access times such as late-night or weekend logins outside typical work hours.
- source_ip (85% populated): IP address from which a login attempt originates; aids in identifying potential geographically disparate login attempts.
- destination_host (70% populated): The hostname of the endpoint being accessed; important for understanding resource access patterns across different parts of the network.
