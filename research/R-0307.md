## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries may abuse the CrowdStrike Falcon Platform's dynamic vulnerability management system by exploiting delays or misconfigurations in prioritizing and applying security updates. They can also leverage the platform's reliance on accurate asset context, adversary intelligence, and attack path analysis to identify potential weaknesses that are not yet fully protected.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries exploit delayed application of critical security patches due to risk-based prioritization, targeting systems with less immediate attention.
- Misuse of Falcon Platform's dynamic vulnerability management can be observed through unauthorized access or manipulation of configuration settings related to update policies.
- Indicators include unusual patterns in patching activities, such as a sudden halt or slowing down of updates for high-risk assets.
- Adversaries may use reconnaissance tools to gather detailed information about the security posture and asset context managed by Falcon Platform, looking for specific vulnerabilities to exploit.
