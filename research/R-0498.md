## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries with privileged network access can perform adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) attacks to manipulate network traffic in real-time, enabling them to intercept and modify data flows between legitimate entities. This technique allows adversaries to eavesdrop on communications or inject malicious content into the data stream.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries may exploit vulnerabilities in routing protocols or DNS infrastructure to redirect traffic through their controlled systems.
- Unusual network traffic patterns, such as unexpected redirections and encryption anomalies, can indicate AiTM attacks.
- Encryption downgrade attacks are a common method used by adversaries to weaken the security of data in transit during an AiTM attack.
- Tools like SSLstrip or ARP spoofing tools may be employed to facilitate AiTM attacks by intercepting and altering HTTPS traffic.
