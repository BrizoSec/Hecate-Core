## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries may exploit vulnerabilities in third-party environments where Falcon Exposure Management is newly available or use credential theft to gain unauthorized access. They could also leverage the increasing reliance on AI by targeting the AI attack surface as managed by CrowdStrike's Falcon AIDR.

### Key Findings
- Credential theft remains a favored technique for adversaries, highlighting the importance of securing user credentials and monitoring for suspicious activity related to credential misuse.
- The expansion of third-party environment support with Falcon Exposure Management presents new opportunities for attackers to exploit vulnerabilities that may not yet be fully secured or monitored.
- Adversaries might attempt to circumvent AI-powered detection mechanisms like IOAs by developing more sophisticated attack patterns or using machine learning techniques themselves to outmaneuver defensive systems.
- Given the focus on AI safety and security within the Open Secure AI Alliance, there is a growing risk that adversaries will target the infrastructure or data used in training AI models for cybersecurity purposes.
