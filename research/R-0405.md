## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries may exploit the open nature of AI models and harnesses to conduct prompt injection attacks, which manipulate user inputs to induce unintended behaviors in AI systems. CrowdStrike's new taxonomy provides a comprehensive guide on how these attacks work and how they can be concealed, posing significant risks to cybersecurity.

### Key Findings
- Prompt injection attacks allow adversaries to exploit AI systems by manipulating input prompts to achieve unauthorized outcomes.
- The development of the Prompt Injection Taxonomy offers security teams an enhanced understanding of attack vectors and evasion techniques.
- CrowdStrike's participation in the Open Secure AI Alliance highlights the importance of collaborative research for identifying vulnerabilities and developing robust defenses against AI-driven threats.
- Third-party environments are increasingly targeted, as seen with Falcon Exposure Management, indicating a shift towards broader ecosystem attacks leveraging AI technologies.
