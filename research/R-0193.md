## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries abuse the ClickFix technique by tricking users into executing commands that appear to fix computer issues, which in reality deploy malware such as the ARECHCLIENT2 payload. This method is employed by various crimeware strains including Lampion, Lumma Stealer, and NetSupport RAT.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries use social engineering tactics to deceive victims into running malicious commands that install information-stealing malware.
- The ClickFix technique leads to the deployment of a memory-resident payload called ARECHCLIENT2 (SECTOPRAT) by A Wretch Client, which steals banking and sensitive information from compromised systems.
- Multiple crimeware strains such as Lampion, Lumma Stealer, and NetSupport RAT are leveraging the ClickFix method to enhance their social engineering attacks and evade detection.
- Attackers often present deceptive prompts or alerts that appear to be legitimate system warnings, enticing victims to execute commands that compromise their security.
