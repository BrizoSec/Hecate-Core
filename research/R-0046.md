## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries like APT-C-36 use adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) techniques to manipulate network traffic, often by exploiting privileged access or employing ARP poisoning and proxy usage. These methods enable them to intercept and modify data in real-time, facilitating further attacks such as network sniffing and data exfiltration.

### Key Findings
- APT-C-36 leverages AiTM tactics to alter network communications between devices.
- Common AiTM techniques include ARP poisoning and establishing proxies.
- Detection involves monitoring for unusual traffic patterns indicative of manipulation or interception.
- Tools like hvnc are used by APT-C-36 to facilitate remote control and data exfiltration.
