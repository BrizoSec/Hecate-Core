## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries increasingly target AI systems by exploiting vulnerabilities in machine learning models and their operational environment. They leverage advanced techniques to evade detection and manipulate data, aiming to undermine the effectiveness of automated threat hunting and response mechanisms.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries use sophisticated tools like adversarial neural networks to generate attacks that specifically bypass or overwhelm AI-driven security systems.
- Attackers exploit model training datasets by injecting malicious samples to degrade detection accuracy over time.
- Indicators such as unusual patterns in feature importance scores from machine learning models, and anomalous behavior in network traffic that mimics benign activities but triggers specific alerts can suggest adversarial activity.
- Adversaries craft campaigns designed to test the limits of AI SOC capabilities through orchestrated multi-vector attacks, combining traditional methods with novel AI-based tactics.

- event.action (80% populated): Specifies the action performed by the process, e.g., 'create', 'execute'.
- process.name (100% populated): Name of the executable creating the new process, here `powershell.exe`.
