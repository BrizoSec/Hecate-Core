## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries exploited the ProxyShell vulnerability to perform a pass-the-hash attack, gaining unauthorized access and deploying ransomware on Krawczyk Industries Limited's network. The attackers used this method to escalate privileges and maintain persistence within the compromised environment.

### Key Findings
- The exploitation of the ProxyShell vulnerability allowed adversaries to gain initial foothold in the network.
- Pass-the-hash technique was employed for lateral movement and privilege escalation, enabling full control over affected systems.
- Ransomware deployment indicates the attackers' intent to disrupt operations and demand ransom payments from Krawczyk Industries Limited.
- Indicators of compromise include unusual PowerShell activity related to credential dumping and network scans targeting known ProxyShell vulnerabilities.
- The attack highlights the importance of timely patching and monitoring for suspicious activities indicative of pass-the-hash exploitation.
