## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries with privileged network access exploit the Adversary-in-the-Middle (AiTM) technique by intercepting and modifying real-time network traffic, often using methods like ARP poisoning or proxy servers to alter data flows and inject malicious content into the communication stream between devices.

### Key Findings
- ArpPoisoning is commonly used as a method to conduct AiTM attacks in ICS environments.
- Encryption can significantly mitigate the risk of successful AiTM attacks by ensuring data integrity and confidentiality.
- Tools like Ettercap or Cain & Abel are frequently employed for performing ARP poisoning to enable AiTM scenarios.
- AiTM techniques are leveraged across various platforms, including mobile devices, through malicious applications that intercept network communications.
