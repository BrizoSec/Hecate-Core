## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Ransomware actors are increasingly leveraging Rclone for data exfiltration and employing various techniques such as protocol downgrading, living-off-the-land binaries, and process injection to evade detection. They also use known vulnerabilities like PrintNightmare and tools like Cobalt Strike for lateral movement within networks.

### Key Findings
- Ransomware actors utilize Rclone for efficient data exfiltration by configuring it with deleted configuration files.
- Adversaries downgrade protocols, such as SSL/TLS to older insecure versions, to bypass modern security measures and evade detection.
- Living-off-the-land techniques are prevalent, including the use of built-in Windows tools like WMIEXEC.py for lateral movement and PowerShell scripts for executing malicious commands.
- Cobalt Strike is frequently employed by ransomware groups to establish beacons on compromised systems, enabling further exploitation.
- Known vulnerabilities, such as PrintNightmare (CVE-2021-34527), are exploited to gain initial foothold or escalate privileges within a network.
