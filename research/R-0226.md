Endpoint logs should also include process creation events with associated command-line arguments to identify any unusual activity matching known indicators of compromise (IOCs).

### Key Fields
- network.flow.src_ip: IP addresses from which potentially malicious traffic originates.
- network.http.request.uri: URLs accessed by endpoints, which may indicate attempts to establish connections with malicious servers or download payloads.
- endpoint.process.create.cmdline: Command-line arguments used when creating processes that match known IOCs or exhibit unusual patterns indicative of malware activity.
- dns.query.name: DNS queries made by endpoints, particularly those resolving to known malicious domains or IP addresses.
