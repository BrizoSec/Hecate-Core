### Summary
MISP is an open-source threat intelligence platform designed for the automated sharing and correlation of indicators of compromise (IOCs) among security professionals. ### Key Findings

- The platform is deployed on Flatcar Linux, an immutable OS that ensures a resilient environment for managing and sharing cyber threat data.
- MISP integrates various web services like URL shorteners, VPN IP addresses, and others as labels to enrich the context of shared IOCs.
