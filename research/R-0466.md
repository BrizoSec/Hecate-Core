## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries exploit AI systems through techniques like prompt injection, manipulating the inputs to induce unintended behaviors or extract sensitive information. CrowdStrike tracks over 180 different methods of prompt injection and is working with the Open Secure AI Alliance to enhance AI security and safety.

### Key Findings
- Prompt injection is a common technique used by adversaries to manipulate AI systems into performing unauthorized actions.
- CrowdStrike has identified more than 180 unique methods for prompt injection, indicating the widespread nature of this attack vector.
- The Open Secure AI Alliance aims to collaborate on advancing AI safety and security through shared knowledge and resources.
- Falcon AIDR by CrowdStrike is designed to secure the attack surface of AI systems against various threats including prompt injection attacks.
