This incident highlights the importance of timely patch management and robust multi-factor authentication (MFA) strategies.

### Key Findings
- The attackers leveraged the ProxyShell vulnerability to execute a pass-the-hash technique, compromising system integrity and confidentiality.
- Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) were identified through Maltrail, providing actionable intelligence for threat detection and response.
- Organizations must implement robust multi-factor authentication (MFA) to introduce friction that can prevent adversaries from successful exploitation.
- Timely patch management is critical in mitigating known vulnerabilities such as ProxyShell.
- The integration of MISP with Elastic integrations aids in the dissemination of threat intelligence across the organization for a coordinated response.
