## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries targeting government entities in the Middle East exploit sophisticated phishing techniques and advanced persistent threats (APTs) to gain unauthorized access. They use Open Threat Exchange (OTX) pulses to disseminate indicators of compromise, which are critical for identifying and mitigating these threats.

### Key Findings
- Phishing campaigns are a primary method used by adversaries to initially breach government networks.
- Advanced persistent threat actors employ long-term strategies to maintain continuous access to targeted systems.
- Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) provided through OTX pulses help in identifying and responding to these sophisticated threats.
- Tools such as malware, spear-phishing emails, and backdoors are commonly utilized by attackers to infiltrate government entities.
