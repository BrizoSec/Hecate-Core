## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries are employing the ClickFix social engineering tactic to trick users into downloading and executing malicious software, which is disguised as legitimate quick fixes for common computer issues. This deceptive method leverages Starland RAT and WLDR framework to deploy malware effectively.

### Key Findings
- ClickFix exploits user trust in seemingly helpful 'quick fix' solutions to propagate malware.
- Starland RAT is utilized by attackers to gain unauthorized access and control over compromised systems.
- WLDR framework aids in the deployment of payloads, enhancing the stealth and efficacy of ClickFix attacks.
- The campaign primarily targets users through phishing emails or malicious websites disguised as legitimate support services.
- Indicators of compromise include unusual network communications from known good executables and unexpected registry changes.
