## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
BlackCore operates a commercial influence-for-hire infrastructure that deploys coordinated inauthentic behavior across social media platforms using AI-generated personas and deceptive content. The system functions by providing clients with training and tools to flood platforms with messages, manipulate organic engagement, and counter dissenting narratives through sophisticated digital networks.

### Key Findings
- The organization utilizes a dedicated web infrastructure hosted on the domain blackcore[.]online, including subdomains like demo[.]blackcore[.]online for client demonstrations and api/auth subdomains for account management.
- Campaigns involve the creation of fake social media personas with AI-generated profile pictures and deployment across platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok to achieve discourse dominance or counter-narratives.
- Operational evidence includes specific training programs for government clients (e.g., Angolan officials) that resulted in supervised influence operations producing high-engagement content, such as posts receiving approximately 20,000 likes.
- The infrastructure supports password-protected sections (e.g., photos[.]blackcore[.]online, full-report[.]blackcore[.]online) for delivering client materials, with documentation indicating Hebrew-language development origins.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
BlackCore, an Israeli influence-for-hire company, provides state actors with sophisticated digital manipulation services including discourse dominance, organic engagement manipulation, and counter-operations using AI-generated content and fake personas across major social media platforms. Their infrastructure allows clients to deploy coordinated inauthentic behavior, such as the supervised training of government employees in Angola and interference in elections in France, New York, Scotland, Togo, and Angola.

### Key Findings
- BlackCore advertises three primary service pillars: Discourse Dominance (flooding platforms with coordinated messages), Organic Engagement (achieving real people's attention), and Counter Operations (disrupting dissenting narratives).
- The company utilizes a domain infrastructure including `blackcore.online` and subdomains like `demo.blackcore.online` (now offline) to present capabilities, login portals, and client-specific campaign plans (e.g., `angola-plan.blackcore.online`).
- In Angola, BlackCore conducted a 14-week training program for government employees involving the creation of fake social media personas, AI-generated profile pictures, and coordinated amplification on Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok.
- BlackCore has been linked to interference in multiple political events, including the 2026 French municipal elections (confirmed by Viginum), as well as operations in New York, Scotland, Togo, and Angola.
- The operation involved fake news outlets such as 'Agita News' and utilized technical indicators like Hebrew EXIF metadata in logo files to identify the Israeli origin of the company's development.
