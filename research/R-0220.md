## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries exploited Krawczyk Industries Limited using adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) techniques and pass-the-hash attacks, capitalizing on network vulnerabilities to intercept and manipulate traffic in real time. The attack leveraged the ProxyShell vulnerability to gain unauthorized access.

### Key Findings
- The use of adversary-in-the-middle techniques allowed adversaries to modify network traffic in real-time.
- Pass-the-hash attacks were employed to exploit existing vulnerabilities within the network, granting unauthorized access.
- The attackers utilized the ProxyShell vulnerability to enhance their exploitation efforts.
- Monitoring execution points and gathering indicators can help detect such sophisticated attack methods.

## 5. Research Synthesis

### Executive Summary
Krawczyk Industries Limited was targeted using AiTM, pass-the-hash attacks, and the ProxyShell vulnerability to intercept and manipulate network traffic in real-time. MISP logs indicate that monitoring of specific telemetry data points can help detect such sophisticated attack methods.

### Recommended Hypothesis
> Adversaries use adversary-in-the-middle techniques and pass-the-hash attacks to exploit vulnerabilities and gain unauthorized access on Krawczyk Industries Limited networks.

### Gaps Identified
- There is a lack of detailed analysis on how adversaries are using the ProxyShell vulnerability in conjunction with AiTM and pass-the-hash attacks, which could provide deeper insights into their tactics.
- The current telemetry coverage does not fully address the detection of hidden scheduled tasks with SYSTEM-level privileges, which may be utilized by attackers for persistence and lateral movement.

### Key Findings
- Hypothesis: Adversaries use adversary-in-the-middle techniques and pass-the-hash attacks to exploit vulnerabilities and gain unauthorized access on Krawczyk Industries Limited networks.
- Gap: There is a lack of detailed analysis on how adversaries are using the ProxyShell vulnerability in conjunction with AiTM and pass-the-hash attacks, which could provide deeper insights into their tactics.
- Gap: The current telemetry coverage does not fully address the detection of hidden scheduled tasks with SYSTEM-level privileges, which may be utilized by attackers for persistence and lateral movement.
- Focus: Enhance network monitoring to detect real-time modifications in traffic patterns indicative of AiTM activities.
- Focus: Strengthen incident response procedures to quickly identify and mitigate pass-the-hash attacks.
