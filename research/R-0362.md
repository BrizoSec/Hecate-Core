## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries may exploit vulnerabilities prioritized by the CrowdStrike Falcon Platform to target U.S. government organizations, focusing on those with higher true business/mission risk as determined by the platform's dynamic risk assessment capabilities rather than static CVSS scores.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries use sophisticated attack paths and exploit likelihood analysis to identify and prioritize vulnerabilities that maximize impact within targeted environments.
- CrowdStrike Falcon Platform's integration with Falcon Next-Gen SIEM allows for real-time identification of new threats through IOAs (Indicator of Attack) updates, which adversaries must constantly adapt against.
- Falcon Onum enhances detection capabilities by providing high-impact use cases tailored to federal agencies, helping identify and mitigate advanced persistent threat (APT) activities effectively.
- Adversaries may attempt to exploit third-party EDR tools supported by Falcon Next-Gen SIEM, such as Microsoft Defender, to gain footholds in protected networks.
- Dynamic risk assessment models used by the platform can be bypassed or misused if adversaries understand and replicate its criteria for high-risk vulnerabilities.
- The CrowdStrike Falcon Platform's comprehensive protection measures include cloud security enhancements that must be continuously monitored and updated against emerging AI-driven supply chain attacks.
