## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries abuse AI systems through prompt injection techniques to manipulate or extract sensitive data, exploiting vulnerabilities in the AI's design and training. CrowdStrike tracks over 180 such methods to protect against these threats using their AIDR platform.

### Key Findings
- Prompt injection allows attackers to modify AI-generated content by altering input prompts, leading to unauthorized access and data exfiltration.
- Adversaries use sophisticated social engineering tactics to trick users into providing malicious inputs that compromise AI systems.
- AI models can be manipulated to generate phishing emails or spread misinformation via compromised platforms.
- The threat landscape includes a wide range of techniques like model poisoning, where attackers corrupt training datasets to degrade system performance.
