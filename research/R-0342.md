## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
The CrowdStrike Falcon Platform helps U.S. government agencies comply with CISA Binding Operational Directive (BOD) 26-04 by leveraging real-time behavioral detection, risk-based prioritization of security updates, and integration with the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. It also supports continuous exposure management to ensure systems are protected against known vulnerabilities.

### Key Findings
- The platform uses dynamic models that focus on exploitability to prioritize security updates based on risk.
- Falcon Platform integrates with the CISA KEV catalog to provide real-time detection and remediation of vulnerabilities.
- Continuous exposure management is offered to ensure compliance with BOD-26-04 by monitoring for known exploited vulnerabilities.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries may abuse the CrowdStrike Falcon Platform's risk-based vulnerability management system by targeting assets with high business impact but lower CVSS scores, exploiting a gap in traditional prioritization methods. They can also leverage false positives and delays in dynamic updates to gain an initial foothold or maintain persistence within a network.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries are likely to exploit weaknesses identified through attack path analysis by compromising less critical systems with high business impact but lower CVSS scores, bypassing static risk assessments.
- CrowdStrike's reliance on dynamic models for threat prioritization can introduce latency, allowing attackers to exploit vulnerabilities before updates can be applied across an organization.
- Attackers may use social engineering tactics or sophisticated malware that evades initial detection by Falcon Platform's IOAs in SIEM systems, thereby exploiting a delay in validation and deployment of security patches.
- Third-party EDR tool integration with Falcon Next-Gen SIEM increases the attack surface for adversaries seeking to exploit misconfigurations or vulnerabilities in Microsoft Defender or other integrated tools.

## 5. Research Synthesis

### Executive Summary
The CrowdStrike Falcon Platform helps U.S. government agencies comply with CISA BOD-26-04 by providing real-time behavioral detection, risk-based prioritization of security updates, and continuous exposure management through integration with the CISA KEV catalog. However, adversaries may exploit weaknesses in these systems to gain initial footholds or maintain persistence within networks.

### Recommended Hypothesis
> Adversaries use social engineering tactics and sophisticated malware to evade detection by Falcon Platform's IOAs in SIEM systems to gain an initial foothold on high business impact targets with lower CVSS scores.

### Gaps Identified
- The latency introduced by dynamic models for threat prioritization allows attackers to exploit vulnerabilities before updates can be applied across the organization, posing a significant risk especially for less critical but strategically important assets.
- The integration of third-party EDR tools with Falcon Next-Gen SIEM increases the attack surface, potentially exposing organizations to misconfigurations or vulnerabilities in integrated tools like Microsoft Defender.

### Key Findings
- Hypothesis: Adversaries use social engineering tactics and sophisticated malware to evade detection by Falcon Platform's IOAs in SIEM systems to gain an initial foothold on high business impact targets with lower CVSS scores.
- Gap: The latency introduced by dynamic models for threat prioritization allows attackers to exploit vulnerabilities before updates can be applied across the organization, posing a significant risk especially for less critical but strategically important assets.
- Focus: Improving real-time detection and response capabilities for network connections and user logon activities to better identify and mitigate data exfiltration attempts or unauthorized access.
- Gap: The integration of third-party EDR tools with Falcon Next-Gen SIEM increases the attack surface, potentially exposing organizations to misconfigurations or vulnerabilities in integrated tools like Microsoft Defender.
- Focus: Enhancing continuous exposure management by leveraging telemetry such as process creation events and file change activities to detect and remediate known exploited vulnerabilities more effectively.
