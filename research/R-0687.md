## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
The threat actor utilized a custom Go loader disguised as a resume from the Beijing Institute of Technology to bypass sandbox detection and deploy SNOWLIGHT, which subsequently downloaded and executed a fileless VShell RAT in memory. This infection chain targeted Chinese defense-tech academics, establishing command-and-control capabilities through C2 infrastructure hosted on Lucidacloud/Cogent networks.

### Key Findings
- The delivery mechanism relied on a Chinese-language executable masquerading as a resume from a Beijing Institute of Technology graduate student, specifically targeting academics in electrical engineering, power systems, or AI research to invoke trust among defense-tech elites.
- The malware employed sophisticated evasion techniques including CPU checks, sleep-timer delays, and sandbox detection within the Go loader before downloading a legitimate DOCX decoy to maintain persistence and reduce suspicion.
- Post-infection, the SNOWLIGHT stager connected to C2 infrastructure (IP 38.207.178.192) to download a 4.65 MB payload which was XOR-decoded with key 0x99 and executed as a fileless VShell RAT, providing interactive terminal access, arbitrary command execution, screenshot capture, and lateral movement capabilities.
- The campaign utilized commodity tooling, specifically cracked versions of the VShell operator platform containing panel-generated SNOWLIGHT components, making precise attribution unreliable beyond identifying an unattributed operator with China-oriented academic targeting.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries utilize fake resumes from Beijing Institute of Technology graduates to target Chinese defense-tech and research professionals, employing a custom Go loader with sandbox evasion to deliver the SNOWLIGHT stager. This chain ultimately installs a fileless VShell RAT on victim systems, leveraging commodity tooling for command execution and lateral movement capabilities.

### Key Findings
- The attack employs a social engineering lure disguised as a resume from a Beijing Institute of Technology graduate student to target mainland Chinese academics in electrical engineering, power systems, or AI research.
- A custom Go loader is used for delivery, featuring sandbox detection, CPU checks, and sleep-timer evasion before downloading a legitimate DOCX decoy file.
- The infection chain utilizes a 1,454-byte SNOWLIGHT stager that connects to infrastructure, downloads approximately 4.65 MB of data, and XOR-decodes it with key 0x99 to launch the final payload.
- The final payload is a fileless VShell RAT providing operators with command execution, file access, screen capture, and lateral movement capabilities.
- Attribution is unreliable regarding specific groups; the operator uses panel-generated SNOWLIGHT components widely available in cracked VShell releases, indicating unattributed actors using commodity tooling.
