## 1. System Research: How It Works

### Summary
Security incident investigations involve analyzing various logs and system data to understand the nature and extent of a breach or suspicious activity. The goal is to identify the root cause, scope, and impact of an incident.

### Key Findings
- Incident investigations often require reviewing multiple sources of information such as network traffic logs, system event logs, and application-specific logs.
- [UNCERTAIN] There might be a need for new or enhanced views that can integrate data from diverse sources to provide a comprehensive picture of an incident.
- The effectiveness of the investigation is highly dependent on the quality and availability of log data before and during the incident.

## 2. Adversary Tradecraft: Attack Techniques

### Summary
Adversaries typically abuse systems by exploiting vulnerabilities, deploying malware, conducting reconnaissance to understand network configurations and access controls, and using various tactics like lateral movement and credential harvesting to gain persistence within an environment.

### Key Findings
- Adversaries often leverage phishing emails or compromised websites to deliver malware initially.
- [UNCERTAIN] Specific tools used by adversaries vary widely but commonly include remote access trojans (RATs) for maintaining control over compromised systems, keyloggers and screen capture utilities for stealing credentials, and custom scripts for lateral movement within networks.
- Indicators of compromise can include unusual outbound network connections, unexpected changes to system configurations or files, and signs of data exfiltration like large volumes of data being sent to unfamiliar IP addresses.
- [UNCERTAIN] Adversaries frequently perform reconnaissance by scanning networks or using social engineering tactics to gather information about the environment before launching a targeted attack.

## 5. Research Synthesis

### Executive Summary
Security incident investigations require the analysis of diverse logs and system data to uncover the root cause, scope, and impact of a breach. Key challenges include integrating data from multiple sources and identifying indicators of compromise such as unusual network activity or changes in system configurations.

### Recommended Hypothesis
> Adversaries use reconnaissance tactics to gather information about networks and systems before deploying malware via phishing emails or compromised websites to achieve persistence and lateral movement within an environment

### Gaps Identified
- The current approach lacks a unified view that integrates data from multiple sources, making it difficult to correlate disparate pieces of evidence during investigations.
- There is uncertainty regarding the specific tools and techniques used by adversaries beyond common categories like RATs or keyloggers, which limits the ability to detect novel threats.

### Key Findings
- Hypothesis: Adversaries use reconnaissance tactics to gather information about networks and systems before deploying malware via phishing emails or compromised websites to achieve persistence and lateral movement within an environment
- Gap: The current approach lacks a unified view that integrates data from multiple sources, making it difficult to correlate disparate pieces of evidence during investigations.
- Gap: There is uncertainty regarding the specific tools and techniques used by adversaries beyond common categories like RATs or keyloggers, which limits the ability to detect novel threats.
- Focus: Develop an integrated telemetry platform capable of consolidating data from various sources into a unified view for more effective incident analysis.
- Focus: Enhance detection mechanisms with signatures and behavior patterns that reflect both known and emerging adversary tactics to improve early warning capabilities.
